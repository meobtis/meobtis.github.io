The *Ḍākārṇava* ("Ocean of the Ḍākas") is classified in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon among the yoginītantras, within the tantra division of the sūtra collection.[^dsbc809] The text available here is short and is treated as a single chapter, divided into numbered sections with page references to the printed edition it was keyed from.[^dsbc809]

The sections consist of verse and short prose passages. The opening addresses a hearer about awakening and the illusory nature of phenomena, and later sections describe mantras and seed-syllables (*bīja*) in terms of their letters and ornaments, together with the practices of meditation, destruction and giving that accompany them.[^dsbc809] The edition on which the digital text rests is credited to Nagendra Narayan Chaudhuri and was published in Calcutta in 1935.[^dsbc809] Little reliable scholarly information on the work's authorship, date, translations or reception could be consulted in preparing this note, so none is claimed here.
