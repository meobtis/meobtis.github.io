The *Samādhisaṃbhāraparivarta* ("Chapter on the Accumulations for Samādhi") is a short Sanskrit text in verse. The digital editions attribute it to Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna, that is Atīśa, and the DSBC text is drawn from a printed collection of eleven works attributed to him.[^gretil-samadhi][^dsbc267] It opens with a salutation to Lokanātha, and its first verses speak of firmly establishing bodhicitta born of compassion, of detachment from worldly enjoyment, of relying on a guru as equal to the Buddha, and of receiving the vase and secret initiations from him.[^dsbc267]

Beyond what the editions themselves show, little reliable information could be gathered about this work: no English title, translation, Tibetan or Chinese catalogue reference, or scholarly study was verified in the course of this research. Its place within the literature attributed to Atīśa, its date and its transmission therefore remain to be confirmed from specialist sources.
