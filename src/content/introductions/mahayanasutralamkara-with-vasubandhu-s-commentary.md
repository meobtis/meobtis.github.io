The *Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra*, commonly rendered "The Ornament of the Mahāyāna Sūtras", is a Yogācāra work in verse with a prose commentary (*bhāṣya*) that runs through the verses. The digital text here contains both the verses and the commentary, which the edition's front matter attributes to Asaṅga and to Vasubandhu respectively.[^gretil-msa] The commentary is known as the *Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkārabhāṣya* and is listed among Vasubandhu's commentaries on major Yogācāra works.[^gold2011]

By Tibetan tradition the root verses are counted among the "Five Treatises of Maitreya", works said to have been revealed to Asaṅga by the bodhisattva Maitreya.[^gold2011] The 84000 project catalogues the Tibetan translation of the verses as Toh 4020, under the heading "Mind-only".[^84000toh4020] The chapter structure begins with a verse introducing the work as an adornment of the Mahāyāna teaching (as seen in the opening of the Sanskrit text).[^gretil-msa]

Beyond what the edition itself records, little further information was found for this entry from sources that could be verified, so questions of date, the relationship between verses and commentary, and the Tibetan and Chinese translations are not treated here.
