The *Prātimokṣasūtra* of the Mahāsāṃghikas is the monastic disciplinary code (*prātimokṣa*) of one of the early Buddhist ordination traditions, in Sanskrit. A *prātimokṣa* is a list of rules for the ordained community, and several such codes survive, among them those of the Theravāda, Mahāsāṃghika and Mahīśāsaka traditions.[^wiki-pratimoksa] The Sanskrit text opens with a salutation and verses in praise of the Buddha's proclamation of the *prātimokṣa*, which extol moral conduct (*śīla*) as the basis of the monk's path, followed by an introductory section (*upodghāta*) in verse.[^gretil-pratimoksa-ma]

Little further information could be verified for this text from the sources consulted. The Mahāsāṃghika school's Vinaya is also preserved in Chinese as the *Mohesengzhi lü* (T. 1425), which according to the account summarised on Wikipedia was brought from Pāṭaliputra by Faxian and translated with Buddhabhadra in 416 CE; the same account lists a *Prātimokṣa Sūtra* among the Mahāsāṃghika Vinaya texts extant in Chinese.[^wiki-mahasamghika] The details of the manuscript on which the 1956 edition rests, and its relation to the Chinese and other versions, are not documented in the sources gathered here.
