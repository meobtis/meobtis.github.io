MEOBTIS indexes the following collections. Each one has been converted to Markdown in
its own repository. **{both}** texts are available from more than one source; they are
listed once in the index, with a link to each edition.

## GRETIL

The [Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html)
(GRETIL) is maintained by the Göttingen State and University Library (SUB Göttingen). It
offers standardised, machine-readable texts in Sanskrit and other Indian languages
contributed by many scholars and projects. Most texts are encoded in TEI XML with full
bibliographic headers.

MEOBTIS includes GRETIL's two Buddhist sections: **Buddhist religious literature** (sūtras,
Vinaya, avadānas, tantras, stotras) and **Buddhist philosophy** (Abhidharma, Madhyamaka,
Yogācāra, Pramāṇa).

- **{src.gretil.editions}** Markdown editions of **{src.gretil.works}** texts
- Markdown repository: [meobtis/gretil](https://github.com/meobtis/gretil)
- Licence: given in each text, mostly
  [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

## DSBC

The [Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon](https://www.dsbcproject.org) (DSBC) is a project of
the University of the West (Rosemead, California), begun in 2003 with the Nagarjuna
Institute in Kathmandu. It digitises Sanskrit Buddhist texts, many of them preserved in
Nepal, and arranges them by the divisions of the canon: Sūtrapiṭaka, Vinayapiṭaka and
Śāstrapiṭaka. Most texts are published in both Roman transliteration (IAST) and
Devanāgarī.

- **{src.dsbc.editions}** Markdown editions of **{src.dsbc.works}** texts, in IAST
  (`DSBC`) and Devanāgarī (`DSBC देवनागरी`)
- Markdown repository: [meobtis/dsbc](https://github.com/meobtis/dsbc)
- Terms: non-commercial educational and research use; see the DSBC
  [usage policy](https://www.dsbcproject.org/pages/usage-policy)
