The *Dharmaskandha* ("Collection of Dharmas") is one of the seven Abhidharma treatises of the Sarvāstivāda school. Its attribution varies: Sanskrit and Tibetan sources name Śāriputra as author, whereas Chinese sources name Mahāmaudgalyāyana. Frauwallner dated it to an early stage of Abhidharma literature, and Yin Shun noted that it is mentioned in the Mūlasarvāstivāda *Vinaya* commentarial literature, which suggests that it entered the Sarvāstivāda canon early.[^wiki-dhskandha] The text was translated into Chinese by Xuanzang as the *Apidamo fayun zu lun* (Taishō 1537) in twelve fascicles.[^wiki-dhskandha]

Little else about the work could be verified from the sources consulted here. The Sanskrit text is known from fragments in the Gilgit manuscripts, edited by Dietz (1984) and supplemented by Matsuda (1986); the opening passage of the digital text comments on the formula "ignorance conditions formations" (*avidyāpratyayāḥ saṃskārāḥ*), in the style of a doctrinal exposition on dependent origination.[^gretil-dhskandha] No established English title was found.
