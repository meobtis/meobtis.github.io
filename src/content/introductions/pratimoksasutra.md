This item is a fragmentary Sanskrit text of a *Prātimokṣasūtra*, the compilation of monastic rules that Buddhist monks recite as part of their communal discipline. The GRETIL record describes it as a Vinaya text in transliteration, based on Lokesh Chandra's 1960 publication of an unpublished fragment, and notes that passages missing or damaged in the manuscript are marked by gaps or ellipses, with restored passages given in italics.[^gretil-prmo]

What survives is arranged by the usual categories of offences: the sections are labelled *pārājika* (Pār.), *saṃghāvaśeṣa* (SA.), *pāyantika* (Pāy.), *pratideśanīya* (Pratid.) and *śaikṣa* (Śai.), and the file also contains *uddāna* verses that summarise groups of rules.[^gretil-prmo] The rule texts in the file follow the formula of the Sanskrit monastic codes, for example opening a rule with "*yaḥ punar bhikṣur*" ("whichever monk"). Beyond what the edition's own record states, little reliable information about this particular manuscript could be gathered for this introduction, so its school affiliation, date and provenance are not discussed here; readers should consult Lokesh Chandra's article for these.
