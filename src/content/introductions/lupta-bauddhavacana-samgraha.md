The *Lupta bauddhavacana saṁgraha* ("Collection of lost Buddhist sayings") is a short Sanskrit compilation held in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon under the category of reference works (*kośa*) within the treatises (*śāstrapiṭaka*).[^dsbc739] The digital text contains verses and short passages grouped under the names of attributed authors and works, among them Ādibuddha, Āryadeva, Jñānapāda, the *Guhyatattvaprakāśa*, the *Caturdevīparipṛcchā*, the *Ḍākinīvajrapañjara* and the *Tattvasaṃgraha*, with one entry on Dauḍīpāda; the headings are those given in the file itself.[^dsbc739-md]

The edition is credited to Vajravallabha Dvivedi, and the digital record gives the source as the journal *Dhīḥ*, published at Sarnath, Varanasi, by the Central University of Tibetan Studies in 1986.[^dsbc739] No English title, date of composition, or discussion of how the selection was made could be found in the sources consulted, and the web search for further documentation was not completed; the work's origin and the status of the quoted passages as otherwise lost in Sanskrit are therefore left undetermined here.
