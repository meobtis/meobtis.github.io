The *Pañcatathāgatastutigāthā* ("verses in praise of the five Tathāgatas") is a very short hymn of about five verses in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon. It opens with a salutation to the five Buddhas (*oṃ namaḥ śrīpañcabhūtāntāya*) and then proceeds through a series of salutations, each addressing the Buddha with a different epithet, such as "Buddha of desire" (*buddhakāma*), "Buddha of joy" (*buddhamoda*), "Buddha of laughter" (*buddhahāsa*) and "Buddha of speech" (*buddhavāca*), before closing with homage to the body of wisdom (*jñānakāya*).[^dsbc165]

The Romanized edition names as its source the anthology *Bauddha Stotra Saṃgraha*, edited by Janardan Shastri Pandey and published by Motilal Banarsidass in Varanasi in 1994.[^dsbc165] Little independent information about this hymn could be found: no study of it, no information on its author or date, no manuscript evidence and no verified Tibetan or Chinese translation were located. How the hymn relates to the five-Tathāgata system of Vajrayāna Buddhism is therefore not documented here, and its attribution and date should be treated as unknown.
