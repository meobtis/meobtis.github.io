The *Vādarahasya*, also called the *Udayananirākaraṇa* ("refutation of Udayana"), is a short Sanskrit work of Buddhist epistemology and logic that GRETIL attributes to Ratnakīrti. The digital text opens with an invocation to Tārā and begins its first chapter (*adhyāya*) with an argument about the capacity and incapacity (*sāmarthya*, *asāmarthya*) of causes, which is characteristic of Buddhist debates on causation and momentariness. The printed edition on which it is based is named as R. N. Pandey's *Udayananirākaraṇam* (Delhi, 1984).[^gretil-vadarahasya]

Ratnakīrti was a Yogācāra and epistemological (*pramāṇavāda*) author of the eleventh century CE, active, according to one reference article, between about 1000 and 1050 and a pupil of Jñānaśrīmitra. That article lists an *Udayanīrakaraṇam* among his works, citing McDermott's study and Thakur's edition of his collected treatises (*Ratnakīrtinibandhāvali*, 1957), but it does not mention the title *Vādarahasya*.[^wiki-ratnakirti] Little further reliable information on this particular work was found, including on its manuscripts, translations or an established English title.
