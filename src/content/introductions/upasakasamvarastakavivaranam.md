The *Upāsakasaṁvarāṣṭakavivaraṇam* ("Commentary on the Eight [Verses] on the Lay Follower's Vows", as the title can be read) is a short Sanskrit work classed under the Abhidharma division of the śāstra literature in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon. The text opens with a salutation to all buddhas and bodhisattvas and to the Sugata, the one who has crossed the ocean of the three trainings, and then turns to the question of the lay precepts, setting aside the standard five training rules in order to distinguish three kinds of person according to how they relate to misconduct and to desire.[^dsbc902]

The digital record gives no author, editor, printed edition or date, and the text is divided into a single chapter.[^dsbc902] In the research available for this entry, no reliable scholarly discussion, catalogue entry or translation of the work could be found, so its authorship, date, tradition and any Tibetan or Chinese counterparts remain undocumented here. The Romanized and Devanagari versions are two renderings of the same transcription.[^dsbc903]
