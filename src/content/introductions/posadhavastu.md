The *Poṣadhavastu* is a section (*vastu*) of the Vinayavastu, the part of the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya that deals with rules of monastic discipline. According to the title of the printed edition on which the digital text rests, it contains regulations for the Buddhist confession ceremony (*Beichtfeier*), the *poṣadha*, in the Vinaya of the Mūlasarvāstivādins.[^gretil-posadha] The Sanskrit text opens with a mnemonic summary (*piṇḍoddāna*) of its topics, and it carries folio references to the Gilgit manuscript (for example folio 53v), so it belongs to the Sanskrit material recovered at Gilgit.[^gretil-posadha]

Little more about this chapter could be verified from the sources consulted, so its detailed contents, date and Tibetan and Chinese counterparts are not described here. The Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya as a whole is extant in Tibetan (a ninth-century translation) and Chinese (an eighth-century translation), while the Sanskrit survives only in part.[^wiki-msv] The edition record notes that the Vinayavastu manuscript is reproduced in the facsimile edition of the Gilgit Buddhist Manuscripts, although folios 43 to 53 of the Stein collection, which cover the Pravrajyāvastu and Poṣadhavastu, are not reproduced there.[^gretil-posadha]
