The *Viśālāmalavatī Pramāṇasamuccayaṭīkā* is a Sanskrit commentary on the *Pramāṇasamuccaya* ("Compendium of Epistemology"), the principal work of the Buddhist logician Dignāga. The Sanskrit text of Dignāga's work was long regarded as lost and survives otherwise in two Tibetan translations; a manuscript of Jinendrabuddhi's commentary was later discovered, and scholars have used it to reconstruct the Sanskrit of the root text, which the commentary embeds.[^wikidignaga] The commentary is traditionally associated with Jinendrabuddhi, but that attribution and the work's date are not documented in the sources consulted here.

The text in this index opens with the second chapter, where the commentator explains that there are only two means of knowledge, perception and inference, and that perception has already been treated; he then turns to inference, distinguishing inference for oneself from inference for others.[^dsbc864] The edition records name Horst Lasic, Helmut Krasser and Ernst Steinkellner as editors of a printed edition published in 2012.[^dsbc864][^dsbc865] Little else about the work could be verified in the reliable sources available during this research, so further details of its manuscript, translations and reception are omitted.
