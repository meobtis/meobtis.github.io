The *Sphuṭārthā Śrīghanācārasaṃgrahaṭīkā* is a Sanskrit commentary (*ṭīkā*) attributed to Jayarakṣita on the *Śrīghanācārasaṃgraha*, a work concerning the conduct (*ācāra*) of novice monks (*śrāmaṇera*). The edition's record identifies the author as Jayarakṣita and the base text as a compendium on novice discipline.[^gretil-sphutartha] The commentary opens with homage to the Buddha and a verse stating that the commentary is composed to aid memory of the compendium by condensing longer sūtra material; it then proceeds verse by verse, glossing terms and explaining how each verse of the root text is to be read.[^gretil-sphutartha]

Little reliable information on the work, its author's date or its translations could be consulted for this introduction, and none is asserted here. Readers should consult the printed edition and catalogues of Tibetan and Sanskrit Vinaya literature for questions of date, authorship and transmission.
