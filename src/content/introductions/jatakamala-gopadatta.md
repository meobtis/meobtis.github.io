This *Jātakamālā* ("Garland of Birth Stories") is the work of Gopadatta, a figure of whom little is documented in the sources consulted. Like the better-known *Jātakamālā* of Āryaśūra, it presents tales of the Buddha's previous lives in elegant Sanskrit, but it is a distinct text. Encyclopaedic overviews describe Āryaśūra's collection as influential and imitated by later authors, chiefly Haribhaṭṭa and Gopadatta.[^wikipedia-jatakamala] The English title above is a literal rendering and not an established name for this particular work.

The digital text is not a single continuous manuscript text but a compilation of modern editions of individual stories, numbered in the file and including, for example, Ajātaśatru, the Kapīśvarajātaka, the Nāgajātaka, Maitrakanyaka and Saptakumārikā. Some of these are written in the mixed prose-and-verse campū style. The editions named in the file are by Michael Hahn, Ratna Handurukande and Konrad Klaus.[^gretil-gopadatta] Little reliable further information on the author's date, the work's transmission or its translations could be verified, and none is claimed here.
