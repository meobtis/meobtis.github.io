The *Tarkasopāna* ("Steps of Reasoning") is a Sanskrit work on Buddhist epistemology and logic (*pramāṇa*) that GRETIL ascribes to Vidyākaraśānti.[^gretil-tarkasopana] The text opens with a homage to Mañjuśrī and a verse invoking Hevajra, and then proceeds in numbered sections.[^gretil-tarkasopana] It begins by defining a means of valid cognition (*pramāṇa*) as non-deceptive cognition and divides it into two kinds, perception (*pratyakṣa*) and inference (*anumāna*). The definitions are accompanied by grammatical analyses of the terms, and the author cites the Buddhist logician Dharmottara by name.[^gretil-tarkasopana]

Little reliable information on the work's date, transmission or reception could be found in the sources consulted for this introduction, so no claims are made here about the author's dates, the work's Tibetan or Chinese translations, or its manuscripts. The only edition documented in this index is Tucci's, which is described in the edition note above.
