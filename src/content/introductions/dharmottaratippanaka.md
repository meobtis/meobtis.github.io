The *Dharmottaraṭippanaka* is a Sanskrit commentary in the form of a *ṭippaṇa*, a running gloss on selected words and phrases, on the *Nyāyabinduṭīkā* of Dharmottara. The *Nyāyabinduṭīkā* in turn comments on Dharmakīrti's *Nyāyabindu*, a short handbook of Buddhist epistemology and logic. The text opens with the invocation "namo jinamatāya" and proceeds lemma by lemma, each gloss being keyed to the page and line of the Sanskrit *Nyāyabinduṭīkā* (abbreviated "NBT"), beginning with the opening verse of the *Nyāyabindu* on right knowledge (*samyagjñāna*).[^sarit-dtp]

The reference list that accompanies the digital text names the work as by Mallavādin and edited by Yaita in 2005; it also lists the sources the editor drew on, among them Dharmakīrti's *Hetubindu* and *Pramāṇavārttika*, Dignāga's *Pramāṇasamuccaya*, Durvekamiśra's *Dharmottarapradīpa* and an anonymous *Tātparyanibandhanaṭippana*.[^sarit-dtp] Dharmottara is commonly dated to the eighth century (about 740–800), and his *Nyāyabinduṭīkā* is the one of his works that survives in Sanskrit.[^wiki-dharmottara] Little further about the *ṭippaṇa*, such as its date, its manuscript tradition or any translation, could be established from the sources consulted, so those points are left open here.
