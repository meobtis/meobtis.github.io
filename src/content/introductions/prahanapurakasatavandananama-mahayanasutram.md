The *Prahāṇapūrakaśatavandanānāma mahāyānasūtram* ("Mahāyāna sūtra called the hundred homages that complete the abandonment") is a short text catalogued among the Mahāyāna sūtras of the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon.[^dsbc906] From the text itself, it opens with a series of homages (*namaḥ*, "homage to ...") addressed to the buddhas of the ten directions and the three times, naming individual tathāgatas such as Ratnasambhava, Amitābha, Akṣobhya, Śākyamuni and Vairocana, then the past, present and future buddhas, the Dharma and the divisions of the scriptures, a list of bodhisattvas (including Avalokiteśvara, Maitreya, Mañjuśrī and Samantabhadra), and the principal disciples such as Śāriputra, Maudgalyāyana and Ānanda, and the Saṅgha.[^dsbc906] It then continues in verse.

Little reliable information about this work could be found beyond the edition itself: no author, date, place of composition, manuscript source, translation or English title was established, and the DSBC record names no printed edition.[^dsbc906] The text's identity as a distinct work, its relation to similar homage and confession liturgies, and any Tibetan or Chinese counterparts therefore remain to be investigated.
