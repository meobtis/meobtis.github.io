The *Bhikṣuṇīkarmavācanā* is a Sanskrit Vinaya text, that is, a collection of the formulae (*karmavācanā*) recited in the formal acts of a community of Buddhist nuns. No established English title is known to me. The digital text opens with a narrative of Mahāprajāpatī Gautamī asking the Buddha three times to allow women to go forth, Ānanda's intercession, and the Buddha's laying down of the eight weighty rules (*gurudharma*) for nuns, which are followed by further procedural material.[^gretil-bhikkarma]

Little reliable information on the work's authorship, date or school could be found. A catalogue entry for the Oxford manuscript on which the edition rests describes it as a fragmentary palm-leaf manuscript of the second part of a Mūlasarvāstivāda *Bhikṣuṇīkarmavācanā*, dated to roughly the eleventh to thirteenth century; this description was seen only in a search summary of that record and should be checked against the catalogue itself.[^bodleian] The edition appears in the literature as Schmidt's article on manuscript Sansk. c. 25 (R), and the digital text carries the manuscript's folio markers (for example "BhīKaVā 3a1").[^gretil-bhikkarma]
