The *Abhidharmasamuccayabhāṣya* is a Sanskrit commentary on the *Abhidharmasamuccaya* ("Compendium of the Higher Teaching"), the Abhidharma compendium traditionally attributed to Asaṅga. The text is arranged in the same sections as its root text, beginning with the "compendium of characteristics" (*lakṣaṇasamuccaya*), and it glosses the root text phrase by phrase, explaining terms such as the aggregates, elements and sense-bases and the notion of appropriation (*upādāna*). No standard English title for the commentary appears to be established.[^gretil-abhsbh]

Its authorship is uncertain. The catalogue record of the printed edition describes it as ascribed to Sthiramati or Jinaputra, and notes that it was deciphered from a manuscript that Rāhula Sāṅkṛtyāyana found in Tibet.[^tatia1976] According to a Wikipedia summary of de Jong's review, the Tibetan canon attributes the commentary to Jinaputra and the Chinese canon to a different name, while a separate commentary, the *Abhidharmasamuccayavyākhyā*, survives in Tibetan and Chinese; this account is a tertiary lead and should be checked against de Jong's review.[^wiki-abhs]

Little else about the commentary's date, place of composition or reception was found in sources that could be verified. It is known today chiefly through Tatia's 1976 Sanskrit edition, which has an English introduction.[^tatia1976]
