The *Nyāyapraveśapravṛtti* is a Sanskrit commentary attributed in the edition records to Haribhadrasūri. In its opening verses the author pays homage to the Jina and announces that he is composing a clear explanation (*sphuṭārthā vyākhyā*) of the *Nyāyapraveśaka*, a work on logic that had already been explained at length by others, and that he will give a concise account out of compassion for beings.[^gretil-nyayapravesapravrtti] The commentary then opens by considering why the root text begins with an introductory verse, and answers objections that a work lacking a stated purpose or subject matter should not be undertaken, using the scholastic style of Indian commentaries.[^gretil-nyayapravesapravrtti]

Beyond what the text itself and the edition records show, little reliable information could be gathered for this introduction: the author's identification and date, the commentary's relation to other commentaries on the *Nyāyapraveśa*, and any Tibetan or Chinese translations are not documented here, and readers should consult specialist scholarship before relying on any attribution. The printed edition behind both digital texts is A. B. Dhruva's 1987 Delhi edition.[^dsbc297]
