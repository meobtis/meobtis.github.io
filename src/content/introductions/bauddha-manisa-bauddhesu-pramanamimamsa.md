*Bauddha manīṣā- Bauddheṣu Pramāṇamīmāṃsā* ("Buddhist wisdom: the investigation of the means of valid knowledge among the Buddhists") is a short prose essay in Sanskrit, about 25 KB in its romanized form, held as a single chapter in the pramāṇa section of the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon.[^dsbc719] The catalogue record gives no author, editor, publisher or year, and searches for the title in library catalogues and scholarly literature found no independent record of the work, so its authorship, date and place of publication remain undocumented here.

From the text itself, the essay presents Buddhist epistemology as the culmination of the Buddhist tradition. It discusses the relationship of the Buddhist logicians to the Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika schools, argues for the importance of wisdom (*prajñā*) in Buddhist thought, and cites authorities such as Nāgārjuna, Dignāga, Dharmakīrti (*Pramāṇavārttika*) and Prajñākaragupta. It treats the Buddha as a source of valid knowledge and discusses the two kinds of valid cognition and the debate between realist and idealist (*vijñānavāda*) accounts of the object of knowledge.[^dsbc719] A Devanagari version is held as a separate record.[^dsbc720]
