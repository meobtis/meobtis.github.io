The *Upasaṃpadājñapti* is a short Sanskrit text on the ordination procedure (*upasaṃpadā*) of the Mūlasarvāstivāda monastic tradition. According to the header of the digital edition, it covers the taking of the refuges and precepts, the formal motions (*karmavācanā*) of the ceremony, the questioning of the candidate about obstacles, the four dependences (*niśraya*), and the offences entailing defeat (*pārājika*) and the duties of a renunciant (*śramaṇakaraka*), followed by closing verses.[^gretil-uj] The opening passages give the lay precepts and the formulas the candidate recites, and then the announcement that the assembly makes on the candidate's behalf.[^gretil-uj]

No author is named in the edition, and little reliable secondary literature on the work could be found in the sources consulted. The GRETIL notes give parallel references to the Patna edition of B. Jinananda (abbreviated there as Upj(J)), to a Sanskrit manuscript cited as Skt.C, and to the Tibetan Mūlasarvāstivāda *Kṣudrakavastu*, which shows that the text is closely tied to Mūlasarvāstivāda vinaya ritual material.[^gretil-uj] Its date, place of composition and relation to the other recensions are not documented in the sources read here and are left open.
