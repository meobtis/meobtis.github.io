The *Saṅghabhedavastu* is, according to the title of the printed edition on which the digital text rests, the seventeenth and last section of the Vinaya of the Mūlasarvāstivādins, and it is known from a Gilgit manuscript.[^gretil-sbv] The Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya is one of the three surviving Vinaya lineages, together with those of the Dharmaguptaka and Theravāda schools; it survives in a ninth-century Tibetan translation, an eighth-century Chinese translation and, to some extent, in the original Sanskrit.[^wiki-msv] The title, which may be rendered "the section on schism in the Saṅgha", is not discussed further in the sources consulted here.

Little detailed, reliably citable information about the contents, date and transmission of this section could be consulted for this introduction, and none is asserted here beyond what the edition's own front matter states. The opening lines of the text consist of verse summaries (*uddāna*) listing the narrative episodes of the section, with page references to Gnoli's edition embedded in the text.[^gretil-sbv] Readers should consult Gnoli's edition and specialist literature on the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya for the content and background of the work.
