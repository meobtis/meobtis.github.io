*Bauddhapāribhāṣikaśabdāḥ* ("Buddhist technical terms"; the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon spells the title *Baudha pāribhāṣikāḥ śabdāḥ*) is a short glossary-like text. Its entries are Sanskrit technical terms such as *akṣara*, *akṣobhya*, *acintya* and *acintyadhātu*, listed in alphabetical order, each followed by one or more quotations that illustrate the term and a brief source reference in abbreviated form, for example to the *Sekoddeśaṭīkā* and the *Advayavajrasaṃgraha*, with page numbers.[^dsbc749]

Very little is documented about the work itself. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon records it as a text in the *śāstrapiṭaka* section, in the category of commentaries on the Advaya tantras, and gives its bibliographic source as the journal *Dhīḥ*, edited by S. Rinpoche and Vraj Vallabh Dwivedi and published in 1986 by the Central University of Tibetan Studies in Sarnath, Varanasi.[^dsbc749][^dsbc750] The records name no compiler and give no date of composition, and no further scholarly discussion or translation was found; the work appears to be a modern editorial or lexicographic compilation rather than a classical treatise, but this is an inference from its form, not a documented fact.
