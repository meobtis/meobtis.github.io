The *Dhāraṇī Saṃgraha* ("collection of dhāraṇīs") is a Sanskrit text classed under the dhāraṇī section of the Sūtrapiṭaka in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon. Its record names Atul Bhosekar as editor and gives the year 2017, but no publisher or place of publication, and it offers no description of the work.[^dsbc820]

The text itself opens with a homage to Vajrasattva and goes on to name figures such as Vairocana, Amitābha and Amoghasiddhi, in a Sanskrit that is irregular in places.[^dsbc820] Beyond what the edition records state, no reliable scholarly discussion of this particular collection, its date, compiler, manuscript basis or Tibetan and Chinese parallels could be found, so none is claimed here; it should not be assumed to be identical to other works of similar title.
