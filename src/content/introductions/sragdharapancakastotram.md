The *Sragdharāpañcakastotram* ("hymn in sragdharā verses on the five [Buddhas]") is a short Sanskrit Buddhist stotra. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon (DSBC) files it in its hymn (*stotra*) division under the heading *pañcabuddha*, the five Buddhas.[^dsbc166]

The text consists of a small number of verses in the sragdharā metre, as the title indicates. The opening verse pays homage to the original Buddha (*ādibuddha*), described in terms of the five wisdoms (*pañcajñāna*) and as a mind-shrine (*cittacaitya*) that is formless and pure like space. Later verses offer one's body, speech and mind and the merit of many births, and go on to enumerate the five Buddhas, beginning with Vairocana, Akṣobhya and Ratnasaṃbhava.[^dsbc166]

Little reliable information about the work is documented in the sources consulted. The DSBC record names no author, date or place of composition, and no Tibetan or Chinese translation, manuscript or commentary could be verified. The features of its vocabulary suggest a tantric or Newar-Buddhist devotional milieu, but this is an impression from the text, not an established attribution. Its only known printed source in the DSBC record is the anthology of Buddhist hymns edited by Pandey.[^dsbc166]
