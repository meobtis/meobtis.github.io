The *Pramāṇavārttika Pariśiṣṭa* ("Appendix to the *Pramāṇavārttika*") is not an independent treatise but a set of notes attributed to Vibhūticandra. According to the SARIT record, they were added to a manuscript of Manorathanandin's *Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti*, a commentary on Dharmakīrti's *Pramāṇavārttika*, and were printed as Appendix I of Rāhula Sāṅkṛtyāyana's edition of that commentary.[^sarit-pvp] Manorathanandin's work is one of the Sanskrit commentaries on the *Pramāṇavārttika*.[^wiki-pv]

The notes survive in Sāṅkṛtyāyana's manuscript no. 237, a paper manuscript of 105 leaves written by Vibhūticandra in an early Old Bengali script, which Sāṅkṛtyāyana is recorded as finding on 28 July 1936 at the Zha lu ri phug hermitage.[^sarit-pvp] The text opens by explaining why the author comments on Dignāga's *Pramāṇasamuccaya* through the verse-commentary (*vārttika*) form, and goes on to relate the chapters of the *Pramāṇavārttika* to the *Pramāṇasamuccaya*. Little else about the work, its date or its reception is documented in the sources consulted, and no established English title was found.
