The *Ratnamālāstotram* is a short Sanskrit hymn of praise (*stotra*) addressed to Lokeśvara, that is, the bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files it among its Avalokiteśvara hymns, and its colophon-style heading ascribes it to "Ācārya Vanaratna".[^dsbc132] The text is written in verse; its opening stanzas praise Lokeśvara as one whose mind is moistened with compassion and whose body is pure, and speak of the one bodhisattva appearing in many forms to suit the needs of different disciples, like the moon reflected in many vessels of water.[^dsbc132]

Beyond what the editions themselves record, little reliable information about this particular hymn could be found in the sources consulted for this introduction, and no established English title is known. Its date, the identity of its author, and any Tibetan or Chinese translation or manuscript tradition are therefore not discussed here.
