The *Guhyavajravilāsinīsādhana* (roughly "Sādhana of the Secret Vajravilāsinī") is a Sanskrit work of the tantric Buddhist tradition, catalogued in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon among the commentaries on yoginī tantras.[^dsbc342] No established English title appears in the sources consulted, and little independent scholarship on the work could be found.

The digital text carries a heading attributing it to the "siddhācārya" Śabarapāda and gives the alternative title *Yoginīsarvasva* ("The Whole of the Yoginīs"). It begins with homage to the goddess Vajravilāsinī and to Lokanātha, and its verses present the goddess as a radiant embodiment of passion and bliss; the text then proceeds as a *sādhana*, a ritual and meditative procedure.[^dsbc342] These details are taken from the opening of the digital text only. The authorship, date and place of composition, manuscripts, any Tibetan or Chinese translations and the printed edition behind the digital version could not be verified from the sources available, and the attribution to Śabarapāda should be treated as the text's own claim.
