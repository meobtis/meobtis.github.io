The *Catuḥstavasamāsārtha* ("the summarised meaning of the Four Hymns") is a short Sanskrit work attributed to Amṛtākara. According to its GRETIL record, it is known from Giuseppe Tucci's edition in *Minor Buddhist Texts, Part I* (1956), where it occupies pp. 238–246, and the digital text abbreviates it as "Css".[^gretil-css]

The "Four Hymns" (*Catuḥstava*) are a set of devotional-philosophical hymns traditionally attributed to Nāgārjuna: the *Lokātīta-stava*, *Niraupamya-stava*, *Acintya-stava* and *Paramārtha-stava*.[^wikinag] The text itself is arranged hymn by hymn, with headings such as *Niraupamyastava* and *Acintyastava*, and each section closes with a formula stating that it is the "summary of the meaning" of the corresponding hymn. It introduces the hymns' verses with comments that place them within the stages (*bhūmi*) of the bodhisattva path.[^gretil-css]

Little reliable information on Amṛtākara, the date of the commentary or its transmission could be found in the sources consulted, so these matters are left open here.
