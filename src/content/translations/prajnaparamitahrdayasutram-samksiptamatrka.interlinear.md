# Prajñāpāramitāhṛdayasūtram [saṃkṣiptamātṛkā] (The Discourse of the Heart of the Perfection of Wisdom the concise text)

1.1 prajñāpāramitāhṛdayasūtram|  
🚻⨀①🆎(prajñā+pāramitā+hṛdaya+sūtra)  
discourse on the heart of the perfection of wisdom

1.2 [saṃkṣiptamātṛkā]  
🚺⨀①🆎(saṃkṣipta+mātṛkā)  
concise matrix

The Discourse of the Heart of the Perfection of Wisdom  
the concise text

2.1 namaḥ sarvajñāya||  
🚻⨀①(namas) 🚹⨀④🆎(sarva+jña)  
homage | to the All Knowing  
Homage to the `sarvajña` (All Knowing).

3.1 āryāvalokiteśvarabodhisattvo gambhīrāyāṃ prajñāpāramitāyāṃ caryāṃ caramāṇo vyavalokayati sma|  
🚹⨀①(ārya) 🚹⨀①(avalokiteśvara) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚺⨀⑦(gambhīra) 🚺⨀⑦🆎(prajñā+pāramitā) 🚺⨀②(caryā) 🚹⨀①🔽(māna)(caramāṇa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vyavalokayati) ⏏️(sma)  
ārya | Avalokiteśvara | bodhisattva | in the profound | in the perfection of wisdom | practice | practising | examines closely | (past)  
The bodhisattva ārya Avalokiteśvara, while carrying out the practice in the profound `prajñāpāramitā` (perfection of wisdom), looked closely.

3.2 pañca skandhāḥ, tāṃśca svabhāvaśūnyān paśyati sma||  
🚹⨂①(pañcan) 🚹⨂①(skandha) , *️⃣🤟🚹⨂②(tad) ⏏️(ca) 🚹⨂②🆎(sva+bhāva+śūnya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati) ⏏️(sma)  
five | collections | , | them | and | empty of intrinsic nature | sees | (past)  
There are five `skandha` (collections), and he saw them as `śūnya` (empty) of `svabhāva` (intrinsic nature).

4.1 iha śāriputra rūpaṃ śūnyatā, śūnyataiva rūpam|  
⏏️(iha) 🚹⨀⓪(śāriputra) 🚻⨀①(rūpa) 🚺⨀①(śūnyatā) , 🚺⨀①(śūnyatā) ⏏️(eva) 🚻⨀①(rūpa)  
here | Śāriputra | form | emptiness | , | emptiness | indeed | form  
Here, Śāriputra, `rūpa` (form) is `śūnyatā` (emptiness); emptiness indeed is rūpa.

4.2 rūpānna pṛthak śūnyatā, śūnyatāyā na pṛthag rūpam|  
🚻⨀⑤(rūpa) ⏏️(na) ⏏️(pṛthak) 🚺⨀①(śūnyatā) , 🚺⨀⑤(śūnyatā) ⏏️(na) ⏏️(pṛthak) 🚻⨀①(rūpa)  
from form | not | apart | emptiness | , | from emptiness | not | apart | form  
śūnyatā is not separate from rūpa; rūpa is not separate from śūnyatā.

4.3 yadrūpaṃ sā śūnyatā, yā śūnyatā tadrūpam||  
*️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(yad) 🚻⨀①(rūpa) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(tad) 🚺⨀①(śūnyatā) , *️⃣🤟🚺⨀①(yad) 🚺⨀①(śūnyatā) 🚻⨀①🆎(tad+rūpa)  
what | form | that | emptiness | , | what | emptiness | that form  
Whatever is rūpa, that is śūnyatā; whatever is śūnyatā, that is rūpa.

5.1 evameva vedanāsaṃjñāsaṃskāravijñānāni||  
⏏️(evam) ⏏️(eva) 🚻⨂①🆎(vedanā+saṃjñā+saṃskāra+vijñāna)  
so | just | feeling, apperception, mental construction and consciousness  
So indeed are `vedanā` (feeling), `saṃjñā` (apperception), `saṃskāra` (mental construction), and `vijñāna` (consciousness).

6.1 ihaṃ śāriputra sarvadharmāḥ śūnyatālakṣaṇā anutpannā aniruddhā amalā na vimalā nonā na paripūrṇāḥ|  
⏏️(iha) 🚹⨀⓪(śāriputra) 🚹⨂①🆎(sarva+dharma) 🚹⨂①🆎(śūnyatā+lakṣaṇa) 🚹⨂①🔽(ta)🆎(na+utpanna) 🚹⨂①🔽(ta)🆎(na+niruddha) 🚹⨂①🆎(na+mala) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①(vimala) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①(ūna) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①(paripūrṇa)  
here | Śāriputra | all phenomena | having the characteristic of emptiness | unarisen | unceased | unstained | not | pure | not | deficient | not | complete  
Here, Śāriputra, all phenomena have the `lakṣaṇa` (characteristic) of emptiness; they are `anutpanna` (unarisen), `aniruddha` (unceased), `amala` (unstained), not `vimala` (pure), not `ūna` (deficient), not `paripūrṇa` (complete).

6.2 tasmācchāriputra śūnyatāyāṃ na rūpam, na vedanā, na saṃjñā, na saṃskārāḥ, na vijñānāni|  
⏏️(tasmāt) 🚹⨀⓪(śāriputra) 🚺⨀⑦(śūnyatā) ⏏️(na) 🚻⨀①(rūpa) , ⏏️(na) 🚺⨀①(vedanā) , ⏏️(na) 🚺⨀①(saṃjñā) , ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①(saṃskāra) , ⏏️(na) 🚻⨂①(vijñāna)  
therefore | Śāriputra | in emptiness | no | form | , | no | feeling | , | no | apperception | , | no | mental constructions | , | no | consciousnesses  
Therefore, Śāriputra, in śūnyatā there is no rūpa, no vedanā, no saṃjñā, no mental constructions, no vijñāna.

6.3 na cakṣuḥśrotraghrāṇajihvākāyamanāṃsi, na rūpaśabdagandharasaspraṣṭavyadharmāḥ|  
⏏️(na) 🚻⨂①🆎(cakṣus+śrotra+ghrāṇa+jihvā+kāya+manas) , ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①🆎(rūpa+śabda+gandha+rasa+spraṣṭavya+dharma)  
no | eye, ear, nose, tongue, body and mind | , | no | forms, sounds, smells, tastes, tangibles and phenomena  
There is no eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, or mind; no form, sound, smell, taste, tangible, or phenomenon.

6.4 na cakṣurdhāturyāvanna manodhātuḥ||  
⏏️(na) 🚹⨀①🆎(cakṣus+dhātu) ⏏️(yāvat) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨀①🆎(manas+dhātu)  
no | component of the eye | up to | no | component of the mind  
There is no `dhātu` (component) of the eye, and so forth up to no component of the mind.

7.1 na vidyā nāvidyā na vidyākṣayo nāvidyākṣayo yāvanna jarāmaraṇaṃ na jarāmaraṇakṣayo na duḥkhasamudayanirodhamārgā na jñānaṃ na prāptitvam||  
⏏️(na) 🚺⨀①(vidyā) ⏏️(na) 🚺⨀①(avidyā) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨀①🆎(vidyā+kṣaya) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨀①🆎(avidyā+kṣaya) ⏏️(yāvat) ⏏️(na) 🚻⨀①🆎(jarā+maraṇa) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨀①🆎(jarā+maraṇa+kṣaya) ⏏️(na) 🚹⨂①🆎(duḥkha+samudaya+nirodha+mārga) ⏏️(na) 🚻⨀①(jñāna) ⏏️(na) 🚻⨀①🔽(tva)(prāptitva)  
no | knowledge | no | ignorance | no | exhaustion of knowledge | no | exhaustion of ignorance | up to | no | ageing and death | no | exhaustion of ageing and death | no | dissatisfaction, origin, cessation and path | no | knowledge | no | attainment  
There is no knowledge, no `avidyā` (ignorance), no exhaustion of knowledge, no exhaustion of ignorance, and so forth up to no `jarāmaraṇa` (ageing and death), no exhaustion of ageing and death; no `duḥkha` (dissatisfaction), `samudaya` (origin), `nirodha` (cessation), or `mārga` (path); no knowledge, no attainment.

8.1 bodhisattvaśca prajñāpāramitāmāśritya viharaty acittāvaraṇaḥ|  
🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) ⏏️(ca) 🚺⨀②🆎(prajñā+pāramitā) 🔽(ya)(āśrayati) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) 🚹⨀①🆎(na+citta+āvaraṇa)  
bodhisattva | and | perfection of wisdom | having relied on | dwells | without obstruction of mind  
And a bodhisattva, relying on the prajñāpāramitā, dwells without obstruction of the `citta` (mind).

8.2 cittāvaraṇanāstitvādatrasto viparyāsātikrānto niṣṭhanirvāṇaḥ|  
🚻⨀①🆎(citta+avara+nanā) 🚻⨀⑤🔽(tva)(astitva) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(na+trasta) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)🆎(viparyāsa+atikrānta) 🚹⨀①🆎(niṣṭha+nirvāṇa)  
of obstruction of mind, non- | because of existence | unafraid | gone beyond distortion | having final extinguishment  
Because there is no obstruction of the mind, he is unafraid, has passed beyond `viparyāsa` (distortion), and has `niṣṭhanirvāṇa` (final extinguishment).

8.3 tryadhvavyavasthitāḥ sarvabuddhāḥ prajñāpāramitāmāśritya anuttarāṃ samyaksaṃbodhimabhisaṃbuddhāḥ||  
🚹⨂①🔽(ta)🆎(tri+adhvan+vyavasthita) 🚹⨂①🆎(sarva+buddha) 🚺⨀②🆎(prajñā+pāramitā) 🔽(ya)(āśrayati) 🚺⨀②🆎(na+uttara) 🚺⨀②🆎(samyak+saṃbodhi) 🚹⨂①(abhisambuddha)  
abiding in the three times | all Buddhas | perfection of wisdom | having relied on | unsurpassed | perfect awakening | fully awakened  
All the Buddhas who abide in the three times, relying on the prajñāpāramitā, have fully awakened to unsurpassed `samyaksaṃbodhi` (perfect awakening).

9.1 tasmājjñātavyaḥ prajñāpāramitāmahāmantro mahāvidyāmantro'nuttaramantro'samasamamantraḥ sarvaduḥkhapraśamanaḥ satyamamithyatvāt prajñāpāramitāyāmukto mantraḥ|  
⏏️(tasmāt) 🚹⨀①🔽(tavya)(jñātavya) 🚹⨀①🆎(prajñā+pāramitā+mahā+mantra) 🚹⨀①🆎(mahā+vidyā+mantra) 🚹⨀①🆎(na+uttara+mantra) 🚹⨀①🆎(na+sama+sama+mantra) 🚹⨀①🆎(sarva+duḥkha+praśamana) 🚻⨀①(satya) 🚻⨀⑤🆎(na+mithyatva) 🚺⨀⑦🆎(prajñā+pāramitā) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(ukta) 🚹⨀①(mantra)  
therefore | to be known | great incantation of the perfection of wisdom | incantation of great knowledge | unsurpassed incantation | incantation equal to the unequalled | allaying all dissatisfaction | truth | because of non-falsity | in the perfection of wisdom | spoken | incantation  
Therefore the great `mantra` (incantation) of the prajñāpāramitā should be known: the incantation of great `vidyā` (knowledge), the unsurpassed incantation, the incantation equal to the unequalled, the allayer of all dissatisfaction, the `satya` (truth) because it is not false, the incantation spoken in the prajñāpāramitā.

9.2 tadyathā- gate gate pāragate pārasaṃgate bodhi svāhā||  
⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) 🚺⨀⓪🔽(ta)(gata) 🚺⨀⓪🔽(ta)(gata) 🚺⨀⓪🆎(pāra+gata) 🚺⨀⓪🔽(ta)🆎(pāra+saṃgata) 🚺⨀⓪(bodhi) ⏏️(svāhā)  
namely | gone one | gone one | one gone to the far shore | one gone completely to the far shore | awakening | hail  
It is as follows: `gate` (gone one), `gate` (gone one), `pāragate` (one gone beyond), `pārasaṃgate` (one gone completely beyond), `bodhi` (awakening), hail!

10.1 iti prajñāpāramitāhṛdayasūtraṃ samāptam||  
⏏️(iti) 🚻⨀①🆎(prajñā+pāramitā+hṛdaya+sūtra) 🚻⨀①🔽(ta)(samāpta)  
thus | discourse on the heart of the perfection of wisdom | completed  
Thus the Discourse of the Heart of the Perfection of Wisdom is completed.
