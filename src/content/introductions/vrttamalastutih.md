The *Vṛttamālāstuti* is a Sanskrit hymn of praise (*stuti*) in the *alaṃkāra* (poetics) section of the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon. The edition on which the digital text is based is titled *Vṛttamālāstuti of Jñānaśrīmitra with Śākyarakṣita's Vṛttamālā(stuti)vivṛti*, so the verses are attributed to Jñānaśrīmitra and are accompanied by a commentary (*vivṛti*) by Śākyarakṣita.[^dsbc830]

The text opens with homage to Mañjuśrī, and the commentary reads the first verse on two levels: *vṛtta* is taken both as the "conduct" of Mañjuśrī, in peaceful, partly wrathful and fiercely wrathful forms, and as the "metre" of verse, divided into equal, half-equal and unequal types.[^dsbc830] Beyond what the edition record and the opening of the text show, little reliable information about the work's date, transmission or translations could be found, and none is asserted here.
