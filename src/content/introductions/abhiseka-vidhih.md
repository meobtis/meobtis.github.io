The *Abhiṣeka-vidhiḥ* ("Procedure of Initiation") is a short Sanskrit ritual text of Buddhist tantric practice, filed in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon under works on one's chosen deity (*sveṣṭadevatā*). It consists of a single section, and the digital text reproduces pages 178 onwards of a volume of the journal *Dhīḥ*.[^dsbc889] No established English title appears in the sources consulted, so it is cited by its Sanskrit name.

The opening lines show the nature of the work. After a salutation to Śrī Vajrasattva, the text instructs the officiant on how to prepare one or more disciples, kneeling and holding flowers, to enter a maṇḍala for the sake of attaining the three bodies of a Tathāgata. It then gives verses for the disciple to recite, requesting entry, the pledge (*samaya*), the awakening-mind (*bodhicitta*) and the three refuges, followed by verses of exhortation spoken by the teacher.[^dsbc889] Little else about the work is documented in the sources consulted: its author, date, manuscript basis, and any Tibetan or Chinese translation could not be verified, and are therefore left out.
