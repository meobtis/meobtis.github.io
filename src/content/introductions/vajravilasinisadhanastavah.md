The *Vajravilāsinīsādhanāstava* ("Praise in connection with the sādhana of Vajravilāsinī") is a short Sanskrit stotra, a hymn in verse, that the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon classifies under the stotras of Vajrayoginī.[^dsbc213] The text opens with ornate verses on the sun, the moon and a lotus, and is composed in the elaborate style typical of Sanskrit devotional poetry.[^dsbc213]

Little reliable information about the work could be found: no author, date, place of composition or Tibetan or Chinese translation is documented in the sources consulted, and no established English title is known. The digital text is based on the printed anthology *Bauddha Stotra Saṃgraha*, edited by Janardan Shastri Pandey (Motilal Banarsidass, 1994).[^dsbc213] Readers should treat any attribution or dating with caution until a critical study is available.
