The *Siddhārthacaritakāvyam* ("the poem on the deeds of Siddhārtha") is a Sanskrit verse narrative in ten cantos (*sargas*) on the life of Siddhārtha, the future Buddha. The digital text opens with a heading that calls it a "poem on the career of Siddhārtha" composed by the teacher (*ācārya*) Buddhaghoṣa and names it a great poem (*mahākāvya*) entitled *Padyacūḍāmaṇi*, the "crest-jewel of verses".[^dsbc225] It begins with an invocation of the Buddha followed by a description of the city of Kapila (Kapilavastu), the Buddha's birthplace, in ornate court-poetry style.[^dsbc225]

Little reliable information about the work could be gathered for this entry. The ascription to Buddhaghoṣa and the alternative title are taken from the text's own opening lines, and the identity and date of this author, the place of composition, any translations and any manuscripts or printed editions are not documented in the sources consulted. The name should not be assumed to refer to the well-known Pali commentator of the same name without further evidence.
