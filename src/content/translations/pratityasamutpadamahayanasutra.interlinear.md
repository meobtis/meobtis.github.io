# Pratītyasamutpādamahāyānasūtra

1.1 *(**Vaidya 119**)*  Āryapratītyasamutpādo nāma mahāyānasūtram |  
🚹⨀①🆎(ārya+pratītya+samutpāda) ⏏️(nāma) 🚻⨀①🆎(mahā+yāna+sūtra)  
liberated contingent coarising | by name | discourse of the great vehicle  
*(**Vaidya 119**)* The sūtra of the Great Vehicle, by name Ārya `pratītyasamutpāda` (Contingent Coarising).

1.2 evaṃ mayā śrutam |

1.3 ekasmin samaye bhagavān trāyastriṃśānāṃ devānāṃ madhye viharati sma pāṇḍukambalakalpe śilātale jitāśvādimahāśrāvakaiḥ āryamaitreyāvalokiteśvaravajrahastādibhirbodhisattvairmahāsattvaiḥ aprameyaguṇaratnālaṃkṛtaiḥ mahābrahmasahāṃpatinārāyaṇamaheśvarādibhirdevaiḥ śakreṇa devānāmindreṇa gandharvarājena pañcaśikhena ca sārdham |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑦(eka) 🚹⨀⑦(samaya) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚹⨂⑥(trāyastriṃśa) 🚹⨂⑥(deva) 🚹⨀⑦(madhya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati) ⏏️(sma) 🚹⨀⑦🆎(pāṇḍu+kambala+kalpa) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(śilā+tala) 🚹⨂③🆎(jitāśva+ādi+mahā+śrāvaka) 🚹⨂③🆎(ārya+maitreya+avalokiteśvara+vajra+hasta+ādi) 🚹⨂③(bodhisattva) 🚹⨂③(mahāsattva) 🚹⨂③🆎(na+prameya+guṇaratna+alaṃ+kṛta) 🚹⨂③🆎(mahā+brahman+sahāṃpati+nārāyaṇa+maheśvara+ādi) 🚹⨂③(deva) 🚹⨀③(śakra) 🚹⨂⑥(deva) 🚹⨀③(indra) 🚹⨀③🆎(gandharva+rāja) 🚹⨀③🆎(pañca+śikha) ⏏️(ca) ⏏️(sārdham)

At one time the Bhagavān was dwelling in the midst of the Trāyastriṃśa devas on the stone slab resembling a pale woollen blanket, together with the great śrāvakas headed by Jitāśva; the bodhisattvas, mahāsattvas headed by Āryamaitreya, Avalokiteśvara, and Vajrahasta, adorned with immeasurable jewels of virtues; the devas headed by Mahābrahmā Sahāmpati, Nārāyaṇa, and Maheśvara; Śakra, Indra of the devas; and Pañcaśikha, king of the gandharvas.

⏏️(atha) 🚹⨀①(avalokiteśvara) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(mahāsattva) 🔽(ya)(uttiṣṭhati) 🚻⨀⑤(āsana) 🚹⨀②🆎(ekāṃsam+uttarāsaṅga) 🔽(tvā)(karoti) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(dakṣiṇa) 🚻⨀②(jānu) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(meru+pṛṣṭha) 🔽(ya)(avaṣṭabhnoti) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(yad) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀③(tad) 🚹⨀②(añjali) 🔽(ya)(praṇamati) 🚹⨀②(bhagavat) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀②(etad) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(bravīti) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(etad) ⏏️(hi) 🚹⨀⓪(bhagavat) 🚹⨂①(deva) 🚹⨂①🆎(caitya+namas+kriyā+maṇḍana) 🚹⨂①🔽(ta)🆎(etad+pariṣad+maṇḍala+patita) ⏏️(kathamapi) 🚹⨂①🆎(brahma+carya+puṇya+prasava) 🚹⨀⑦🆎(sa+devaka) 🚹⨀⑦🆎(sa+māraka) 🚹⨀⑦(sabrahmaka) 🚹⨀⑦(loka) 🚺⨂⑦🆎(sa+śramaṇa+brāhmaṇa+prajā) 🚹⨂①(bhikṣu) 🚺⨂①(bhikṣuṇī) 🚹⨂①(upāsaka) 🚺⨂①(upāsikā) 🚹⨂①🆎(subahula+puṇya+prasava) 🚹⨀⑥(bhagavat) 🚺⨀②🆎(dharma+deśanā) 🔵⨂🤟▶️(yācati) ⏏️(iti)

Then the bodhisattva, mahāsattva Avalokiteśvara, rising from his seat, arranging his upper robe over one shoulder, resting his right knee on the surface of Meru, and bowing with joined palms in the direction of the Bhagavān, said this to the Bhagavān: "Indeed, Bhagavān, these devas, having homage to shrines as their adornment, assembled in the circle of this assembly, somehow generating the `puṇya` (merit) of `brahmacarya` (the optimal life), and in the world with its devas, Māras, and Brahmās, among generations with their `śramaṇabrāhmaṇa` (religious practitioners), the bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās generating abundant merit, request a teaching of the Dharma from the Bhagavān."

1.5 tadā bhagavāṃsteṣāṃ pratītyasamutpādagāthāmavocat -  
⏏️(tadā) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂⑥(tad) 🚺⨀②🆎(pratītya+samutpāda+gāthā) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(bravīti)

Then the Bhagavān spoke the verse of contingent coarising to them.

2.1 ye dharmā hetuprabhavā hetuṃ teṣāṃ tathāgato hyavadat |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(yad) 🚹⨂①(dharma) 🚹⨂①🆎(hetu+prabhava) 🚹⨀②(hetu) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂⑥(tad) 🚹⨀①(tathāgata) ⏏️(hi) 🟢⨀🤟↩️(vadati)  
whatever | phenomena | arising from a primary cause | primary cause | of those | Tathāgata | indeed | declared

2.2 teṣāṃ ca yo nirodha evaṃ vādī mahāśramaṇaḥ ||  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨂⑥(tad) ⏏️(ca) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(yad) 🚹⨀①(nirodha) ⏏️(evam) 🚹⨀①(vādin) 🚹⨀①🆎(mahā+śramaṇa)  
of those | and | which | cessation | thus | proclaimer | great śramaṇa

Whatever phenomena arise from a `hetu` (primary cause), the Tathāgata indeed has declared their primary cause,  
and whatever is their `nirodha` (cessation); thus speaks the great śramaṇa.

3.1 yadidamavalokiteśvara ayaṃ pratītyasamutpādastathāgatānāṃ dharmakāyaḥ |  
*️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(yad) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(idam) 🚹⨀⓪(avalokiteśvara) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(idam) 🚹⨀①🆎(pratītya+samutpāda) 🚹⨂⑥(tathāgata) 🚹⨀①🆎(dharma+kāya)  
which | this | Avalokiteśvara | this | contingent coarising | of the Tathāgatas | body of the Dharma  
Namely, Avalokiteśvara, this pratītyasamutpāda is the Dharma-body of the Tathāgatas.

3.2 yaḥ pratītyasamutpādaṃ paśyati, sa tathāgataṃ paśyati |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(yad) 🚹⨀②🆎(pratītya+samutpāda) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati) , *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚹⨀②(tathāgata) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati)  
whoever | contingent coarising | sees | , | he | Tathāgata | sees  
He who sees pratītyasamutpāda sees the Tathāgata.

3.3 yaśca avalokiteśvara kulaputraḥ kuladuhitā vā śraddhāsamanvitaḥ apratiṣṭhite pṛthivīpradeśe āmalakamātraṃ caityaṃ sūcīmātraṃ bodhivṛkṣaṃ bakulapuṣpamātraṃ chatraṃ kṛtvā pratītyasamutpādadharmadhātugāthāṃ paṭhati, sa brāhmaṃ puṇyaṃ prasavati |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(yad) ⏏️(ca) 🚹⨀⓪(avalokiteśvara) 🚹⨀①🆎(kula+putra) 🚺⨀①🆎(kula+duhitṛ) ⏏️(vā) 🚹⨀①🆎(śraddhā+samanvita) 🚹⨀⑦🔽(ta)🆎(na+pratiṣṭhita) 🚹⨀⑦🆎(pṛthivī+pradeśa) 🚻⨀②🆎(āmalaka+mātra) 🚻⨀②(caitya) 🚻⨀②🆎(sūcī+mātra) 🚹⨀②🆎(bodhi+vṛkṣa) 🚻⨀②🆎(bakula+puṣpa+mātra) 🚻⨀②(chatra) 🔽(tvā)(karoti) 🚺⨀②🆎(pratītya+samutpāda+dharma+dhātu+gāthā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paṭhati) , *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚻⨀②(brāhma) 🚻⨀②(puṇya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(prasavati)  
whoever | and | Avalokiteśvara | kulaputra | daughter of a good family | or | endowed with confidence | unestablished | in a spot of earth | size of an āmalaka fruit | shrine | size of a needle | tree of awakening | size of a bakula flower | parasol | having made | verse of the Dharma component of contingent coarising | recites | , | he | of Brahmā | merit | produces  
And, Avalokiteśvara, whatever kulaputra or daughter of a good family, endowed with `śraddhā` (confidence), having made in an unestablished region of the earth a shrine the size of an āmalaka fruit, a `bodhi` (awakening) tree the size of a needle, and a parasol the size of a bakula flower, recites the verse of the Dharma `dhātu` (component) of contingent coarising, that person produces the puṇya of Brahmā.

3.4 itaḥ pracyāvya maraṇakālaṃ kṛtvā brahmaloke utpadyate |  
⏏️(itas) 🔽(ya)(pracyāvayati) 🚹⨀②🆎(maraṇa+kāla) 🔽(tvā)(karoti) 🚹⨀⑦🆎(brahma+loka) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(utpadyate)  
from here | having passed away | time of death | having made | in the world of Brahmā | is reborn  
Having passed away from here and met the time of death, that person is reborn in the world of Brahmā.

3.5 tataḥ pracyāvya kālaṃ kṛtvā śuddhāvāsakāyikānāṃ devānāṃ sabhāgatāyāmutpadyate ||  
⏏️(tatas) 🔽(ya)(pracyāvayati) 🚹⨀②(kāla) 🔽(tvā)(karoti) 🚹⨂⑥🆎(śuddhāvāsa+kāyika) 🚹⨂⑥(deva) 🚺⨀⑦🔽(tā)(sabhāgatā) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(utpadyate)  
from there | having passed away | time | having made | of the Pure Abode host | of the devas | in the company | is reborn  
Having passed away from there and completed their time, that person is reborn in the company of the devas of the Pure Abode host.
