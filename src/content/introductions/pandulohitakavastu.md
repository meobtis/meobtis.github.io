The *Pāṇḍulohitakavastu* is a section (*vastu*) of the Vinayavastu, the part of the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya that deals with rules of monastic discipline. The Sanskrit text opens with mnemonic verses (*uddāna*) listing its subjects: the Pāṇḍulohitaka monks, individuals, those on probation (*pārivāsika*), the suspension of the *poṣadha* rite, lodgings, legal procedures (*adhikaraṇa*) and schism in the community. It carries folio references to the Gilgit manuscript, so it belongs to the Sanskrit material recovered at Gilgit.[^gretil-pandu]

The printed edition on which the digital text rests gives the work the German subtitle "Über die verschiedenen Verfahrensweisen der Bestrafung in der buddhistischen Gemeinde" ("on the various procedures of punishment in the Buddhist community"), which indicates that the chapter is concerned with disciplinary procedures.[^gretil-pandu] Little more could be verified from the sources consulted, so the stories it contains, its date and its Tibetan and Chinese counterparts are not described here.

The Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya as a whole is extant in Tibetan (a ninth-century translation) and Chinese (an eighth-century translation), while the Sanskrit survives only in part.[^wiki-msv] The Gilgit manuscript of the Vinayavastu was edited by Dutt in the 1940s and again, for this chapter, by Yamagiwa, who added the Tibetan text and a German translation.[^gretil-pandu]
