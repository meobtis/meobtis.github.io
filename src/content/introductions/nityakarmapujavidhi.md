The *Nityakarmapūjāvidhi* ("Procedure for the daily rite of worship") is a short Sanskrit ritual manual of Buddhist tantric practice. It begins with homage to Vajrasattva and then sets out the steps of a daily offering rite, including purification by rinsing the mouth with a mantra, the consecration of the offering vessels, the offering of a mandala of the world-system, a hymn of praise and verses on the merit of the rite.[^gretil-nkpv] The DSBC catalogues it among its commentaries on the Yoginī tantras, although the text itself is a liturgical procedure rather than a commentary in the strict sense.[^dsbc349]

Little reliable information about the work is documented in the sources consulted. Its author, date and place of composition are not stated in the digital editions' front matter, and no scholarly discussion, translation or Tibetan or Chinese counterpart could be verified. Both digital editions derive from a single printed edition, prepared by Ngawang Samten and Janardan Pandey and published in a journal of rare Buddhist texts at Sarnath in 2002.[^gretil-nkpv][^dsbc349]
