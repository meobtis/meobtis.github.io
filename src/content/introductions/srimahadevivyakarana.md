The *Śrīmahādevīvyākaraṇa* ("Prophecy concerning the Glorious Great Goddess"; also given as *Āryaśrīmahādevīvyākaraṇa*) is a short Mahāyāna sūtra in Sanskrit. The GRETIL record also preserves the alternative title *Dvādaśadaṇḍakanāmāṣṭaśatavimalīkaraṇā*, and the digital text takes its page numbers (the abbreviation Śmdv) from Dutt's edition of the Gilgit manuscripts, where it occupies about ten pages.[^gretil-smdv]

The text opens with the Buddha staying in Sukhāvatī among a great assembly of bodhisattvas, with Avalokiteśvara and others named. The goddess Śrī Mahādevī approaches him, and he explains to Avalokiteśvara the benefits of upholding her hymn of one hundred and eight names: protection from dangers, and increase of wealth and grain. It then gives a list of tathāgata names, announces that Śrī will in a future age become a buddha in another world-system, lists her epithets, and closes with mantras ending in *svāhā* and instructions for recitation.[^gretil-smdv] Little reliable documentation on its date, authorship, Tibetan or Chinese translations or reception was available when this introduction was written, so these are left unstated.
