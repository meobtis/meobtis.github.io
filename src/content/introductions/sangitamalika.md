The *Saṅgītamālikā* ("Garland of Songs") is a short Sanskrit poem in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon's category of *kāvya*. Its title page describes it as a work "on Yaśodharā" (*yaśodharāviṣaye*), composed by one Yatīndravimala, and it is arranged as a sequence of numbered songs (*saṅgīta*), the first of which is a hymn of praise to Yaśodharā.[^dsbc848] The text as digitised is brief, running to only a few pages.

From the opening of the text, the cycle appears to treat the life of Yaśodharā, the wife of the Buddha: after the opening hymn, its later songs are headed with her birth and her wedding.[^dsbc848] Beyond this front matter and the text itself, little reliable information about the work, its author or its date could be found. The record gives Calcutta, 1960, for the printed source, and no editor other than the title is named, so the work appears to be a modern devotional composition rather than a classical text. No English title, translations or manuscript tradition are documented in the sources consulted.
