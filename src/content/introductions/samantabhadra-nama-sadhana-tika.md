The *Samantabhadra nāma sādhana ṭīkā* is a Sanskrit commentary (*ṭīkā*) on a sādhana, a tantric ritual practice text, concerned with Samantabhadra. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files it in a single chapter under the category of commentaries on the Yoga and Yoginī tantras (*yogottaratantraṭīkā*).[^dsbc756] The text begins with a section headed *ādiyoga nāma samādhi* and opens with a discussion of nirvāṇa, which the commentary supports with a scriptural citation.[^dsbc756]

Little reliable information about the work's author, date, Tibetan or Chinese translations, or manuscript sources could be found in the sources consulted, and no established English title is known. The digital editions state only that they derive from a printed edition prepared by Kimiaki Tanaka and published by Watanabe Publishing, Tokyo, in 2017.[^dsbc756][^dsbc757] Questions of attribution and tradition are therefore left open here.
