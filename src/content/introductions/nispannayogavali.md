The *Niṣpannayogāvalī* ("Garland of Completed Yogas") is a work attributed to the Buddhist scholar Abhayākaragupta, who is generally dated to about 1064–1125 CE and was associated with the monastic centres of north-eastern India. It is described as a manual explaining how to draw twenty-six kinds of maṇḍala, with detailed instructions for each.[^wiki-abhaya] The title's English rendering follows that reference work and is not a fixed convention in the scholarship.

Little further detail on the work's date, its Tibetan and Chinese transmission or its printed editions could be verified from the sources consulted, and none is asserted here. The digital text available here is not a full edition: the GRETIL header records that it derives from R. O. Meisezahl's article on the Mañjuvajra (Akṣobhya) maṇḍala, which presents the Sanskrit of the relevant section, and the text opens with a salutation to Vajrasattva.[^gretil-nispanna]
