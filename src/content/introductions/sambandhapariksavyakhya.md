The *Sambandhaparīkṣāvyākhyā* is a commentary on the *Sambandhaparīkṣā* ("Analysis of Relations"), a short work attributed to the Buddhist logician Dharmakīrti. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy counts it, with the *Saṃtānāntarasiddhi*, among his minor works and notes that its criticism of the Nyāya doctrine of inherence turns in part on the unreality of relations. It also lists Frauwallner's 1934 German edition and translation of the root text.[^tillemans2026] The Sanskrit text opens by testing proposed accounts of a relation (for example, as mutual dependence or as a fusing of forms) and argues, in verse and prose, that none holds up. The opening of the digital text presents it as Dharmakīrti's work "accompanied by a commentary composed by Prabhācandra", but the DSBC record itself names neither author nor commentator.[^dsbc522]

Little else could be verified about this particular commentary from the sources available: its date, its place in the commentarial tradition, any Tibetan or Chinese translation, and the printed edition used for the digital text are not documented in the DSBC record, and further independent confirmation of the attribution to Prabhācandra was not found.
