The *Tarkaśāstra* ("Treatise on Reasoning") is a short Sanskrit text on the conduct of debate. The Sanskrit available today is not a surviving manuscript: it is marked as a "reconstructed text", and its source edition is Giuseppe Tucci's *Pre-Diṅnāga Buddhist Texts on Logic from Chinese Sources*, in which the text occupies pages 1–40.[^gretil-tarka] The title of that volume indicates that the work is known through a Chinese translation and belongs to the Buddhist logic that precedes Diṅnāga. No established English title was found.

Read directly, the text is organised in three sections (*prakaraṇa*)[^dsbc305] and opens as a dialogue in which an opponent's objection that "our statement is unjustified" is turned back on the objector. Its argument turns on the nature of refutation and answer, and it discusses debating faults such as the "points of defeat" (*nigrahasthāna*), for example abandoning a reason in favour of another. I found no scholarly source describing the work's authorship, date, tradition or reception, so these are left open here.
