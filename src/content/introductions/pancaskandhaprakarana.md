The *Pañcaskandhaprakaraṇa* ("Treatise on the Five Aggregates") is a short Yogācāra treatise traditionally attributed to Vasubandhu; it is listed among his works in general reference sources, and the digital editions here name him as author.[^wiki-vasubandhu][^gretil-pks] The text is arranged in numbered sections with headings. It opens by naming the five aggregates (*skandha*): form, feeling, perception, formations and consciousness, and then defines each in turn, beginning with form as the four great elements and what is derived from them.[^gretil-pks]

Little is documented here beyond this. A critical edition of a related Sanskrit work, Vasubandhu's *Pañcaskandhaka*, was published by Ernst Steinkellner and Xuezhu Li in 2008,[^wiki-vasubandhu] but it was not possible to consult that volume, or any study of the text's date, its Tibetan and Chinese translations or its commentaries, so these are left unstated. The text should therefore be checked against the printed edition and specialist scholarship before it is relied on.
