MEOBTIS indexes three sources: GRETIL, DSBC and SARIT (see [Sources](/about/sources/)). Researching what else is
available online turned up the collections below. We do not convert them, either because they are images or print
editions rather than texts, because no licence lets us republish them, or because the Buddhist texts they hold are
already indexed from another source. They are listed here so you can find them.

Descriptions are summaries from each collection's own pages and from announcements by scholars, and may be out of date.
Links were checked in {collections.checked}. If you know a collection that belongs here, or a licence that would let us
include one, please [open an issue](https://github.com/meobtis/meobtis.github.io/issues).
