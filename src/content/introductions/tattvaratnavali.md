The *Tattvaratnāvalī* ("Garland of Jewels of Reality") is a short Sanskrit work of the Buddhist tantric tradition. In the digital edition it is ascribed to Advayavajra. It opens with homage to Vajrasattva and a verse stating that the author will expound the *tattvaratnāvalī*, a clear presentation of reality for people whose vision is obscured by the darkness of having strayed from the proper tradition.[^dsbc790] A section headed *upodghāta* ("introduction") follows.[^dsbc790]

Little reliable information on the work could be gathered when this introduction was written, and the authorship, date and place of composition, any Tibetan or Chinese translation, and the manuscript basis of the edition are not documented in the sources consulted. Readers should consult specialist studies of Advayavajra and of the Sanskrit tantric literature of late Indian Buddhism for these matters.
