The *Niṣpannakrama* ("completion stage") of the Vajrācāryanayottama is a short Sanskrit prose text that the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files under commentaries on mahāyoga tantras.[^dsbc915] Its heading "of the Vajrācāryanayottama" is not explained in the edition record, which gives no author, editor, publisher or year, and little reliable documentation of the work could be found in the sources consulted; no established English title is known.

The text itself begins by stating that the protective rites and similar practices mentioned earlier cannot succeed without the "reality" (*tattva*), which it then identifies with awakening-mind (*bodhicitta*) as unoriginated and tranquil. It supports this with quotations that it attributes to the *Caturdevīparipṛcchā* and to the *Samājottara* explanatory tantra, and goes on to treat the separations of body, speech and mind (*kāya-, vāk-, cittaviveka*), in the manner of the completion stage of the Guhyasamāja tradition.[^dsbc915] The Devanagari edition reproduces the same work.[^dsbc914] Date, author, translations and manuscripts are not documented in the records consulted.
