The *Vajrayoginyāḥ piṇḍārthastutiḥ* ("Hymn on the Condensed Meaning of Vajrayoginī") is a short Sanskrit stotra in sixteen verses. After a brief invocation of Vajrayoginī, the Three Jewels, the guru and the ten wrathful heroes, the verses present the goddess under many epithets and identify her with emptiness, the perfection of wisdom (*prajñāpāramitā*), Nairātmyā and the central channel (*avadhūtī*) of yogic practice. The closing verses say that she grants worldly and spiritual accomplishments and that she is attained through the grace of the guru's feet.[^dsbc216]

The text's own colophon calls it the work of the siddhācārya Virūpa and says the sixteen verses are the "condensed meanings" taken from the *Śrīguhyasamayatantra*, with regard to the three-bodied Vajrayoginī.[^dsbc216] No further information about its date, transmission, manuscripts or translations was found in the sources consulted, so the attribution is reported here only as the text states it, without independent verification.
