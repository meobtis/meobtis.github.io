The *Kalpoktamārīcīsādhana* ("the Mārīcī practice taught in the Kalpa") is a short Sanskrit text in the Kriyātantra section of the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon. Its opening verse pays homage to Mārīcī, described as born of the family of Vairocana, and announces that the sequence of her practice (*sādhanakrama*) will be explained according to the procedure stated in the Kalpa.[^dsbc728]

The text is a ritual manual. It describes how the practitioner, having received the initiation into the Mārīcī maṇḍala, purifies himself, enters the shrine, invites the goddess into her painted image, and worships her with offerings. It gives a mantra for removing obstacles (beginning *oṃ mārīcyai huṃ*), a visualisation of the goddess arising from a seed syllable on a moon or sun disc, offering mantras for flowers, incense and water, and verses of taking refuge and generating the awakening mind (*bodhicitta*).[^dsbc728]

Little is documented about this work in the sources that could be consulted. The edition records name no author, date or place of composition, and give neither the "Kalpa" it refers to nor any printed edition beyond the bibliography entry noted above. No scholarly study of this specific text was found, so its authorship, date and relation to other Mārīcī sādhanas remain unestablished here. No established English title was found.
