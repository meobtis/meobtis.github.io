The *Ḍākinīvajrapañjaraṭippaṇī* is a Sanskrit commentarial work in the form of a *ṭippaṇī*, a brief annotation that glosses selected words and phrases of a base text. Its title indicates that the base text is the *Ḍākinīvajrapañjara* (*Ḍākinīvajrapañjaratantra*), which is listed among the "explanatory tantras" connected with the Hevajra tantra.[^wiki-hevajra] The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon classifies the annotation under the commentaries on yoginī tantras and presents it in two chapter divisions; the text itself marks the sections of the base tantra it follows as successive chapters (*paṭala*), beginning with an invocation of Vajraḍāka.[^dsbc974]

The annotation proceeds by quoting the opening word or phrase of a verse (for example "ākāśeṣu ...") and explaining it, often by identifying the terms with elements of the tantric maṇḍala and of the practitioner's meditation, such as deities, mantra syllables and ritual procedures.[^dsbc974] Beyond this, little reliable information on the work could be found: the author, date, place of composition, Tibetan or Chinese translations and any printed edition are not stated in the edition records consulted, and no scholarly discussion of this particular annotation was located. The summary above therefore rests on the text and its catalogue record only.[^dsbc974]
