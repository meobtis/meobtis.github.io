The *Pramāṇavārttikavṛtti* is the commentary (*vṛtti*) of Manorathanandin on the *Pramāṇavārttika* ("Commentary on Epistemology"), the verse treatise by the Buddhist logician Dharmakīrti. Manorathanandin is named among the commentators on the work in modern scholarship, and his explanations are used in studies and translations of individual passages of the root text.[^tillemans2026] The digital text presents the verses of Dharmakīrti together with Manorathanandin's prose commentary, opening with a salutation to Mañjuśrī and following the work's four chapters, the first of which is headed *pramāṇasiddhi*.[^sarit-pvv]

Little reliable information about Manorathanandin himself, such as his dates, was found in the sources consulted, and none is given here. For the root text, Dharmakīrti's date is debated (sixth or seventh century CE), and the *Pramāṇavārttika* is regarded as an independent exposition of the means of knowledge, which also drew other commentaries and extensive Tibetan scholastic interpretation.[^tillemans2026] The Sanskrit commentary is available in the printed edition by Rāhula Sāṅkṛtyāyana on which the SARIT text is based.[^sarit-pvv] Translation histories of the commentary were not investigated.
