The *Kālacakragaṇitopadeśa* ("instruction on the calculations of the Kālacakra") is a short Sanskrit text in the jyotiḥśāstra (astronomy and astrology) section of the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon. It opens with a prose note that works from a stated epoch date in the Śaka era (year 1091), in a year-count of the Mleccha (Islamic) era and in a sixty-year cycle, and sets out a constant (*dhruvaka*) from which month and year counts are to be computed, relating the "time of Mañjuśrī" to the time of the Tathāgata and to the Mleccha era. It then pays homage to Śrī Kālacakra and states that a "lamp" is being kindled to dispel the darkness of confusion, after which it gives rules for computing the sixty-year cycle.[^dsbc899]

Little is documented about this work in the sources that could be consulted. The edition records give neither author, date, nor printed source, and no scholarly study of the text was found, so its authorship, date and relation to other Kālacakra calendrical literature remain unestablished here.[^dsbc899] No established English title was found.
