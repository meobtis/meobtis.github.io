*Vajrayāna darśana mīmāṁsā* ("An investigation of the philosophy of the Vajrayāna") is a Sanskrit prose work on Vajrayāna Buddhist thought. In the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon it is classed among miscellaneous treatises (*śāstrapiṭaka*, *vividha*), and it is divided into chapters (*paricchedas*), the first headed *vajrayānatattvadarśanam* ("the philosophy of the Vajrayāna truth").[^dsbc832]

The opening chapter begins with the question of how the Buddha's teaching was given, whether in a single turning of the wheel of the Dharma or several, and discusses the traditional scheme of three turnings. It cites sūtra passages, including the *Laṅkāvatāra*, in support. It then turns to the classification of the tantras and their purposes, and the second chapter is devoted to the Kriyātantra, with sections on tantras associated with Mañjuśrī, Avalokiteśvara, Acala and Vajrapāṇi and on the divisions into families (*kula*).[^dsbc832] The edition record names no author, editor, publisher, place or year, and no further reliable information about the work could be found, so its authorship and date remain undocumented here.[^dsbc832]
