The *Vajradevīstotram* ("Hymn to the Vajra Goddess") is a short Sanskrit stotra in verse addressed to the goddess Vajrayoginī, the red, three-eyed, naked dākinī who holds a khaṭvāṅga in her left hand. The text opens by describing her form and ornaments (a garland of skulls, the five mudrās) and then turns to praise her as the mother of all buddhas and bodhisattvas, a giver of liberation, meditative absorption and wisdom, and a destroyer of fears and of the hosts of Māra.[^dsbc212] The text is headed *ācāryanāgārjunaviracitam*, "composed by the teacher Nāgārjuna", an attribution that should be treated as traditional only: the name is attached to many works, and no independent source consulted here establishes the authorship or date.[^dsbc212]

Little reliable information on this particular hymn was available for this entry. Its Sanskrit text is known here only through the printed collection named in the digital edition, and no studies, translations, Tibetan or Chinese counterparts or manuscript descriptions could be verified. Readers wanting more should consult the printed edition and the literature on Vajrayoginī and the Buddhist stotra tradition.[^dsbc212]
