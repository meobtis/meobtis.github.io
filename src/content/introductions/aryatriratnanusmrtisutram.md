The *Āryatriratnānusmṛtisūtra* ("Noble Sūtra on the Recollection of the Three Jewels") is a very short text. In the edition on the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon it is headed by a Sanskrit title and by a Tibetan title transliterated as *phags pa dkon mchog gsum rjes su dran pa'i mdo*, and it opens with a salutation to all buddhas and bodhisattvas.[^dsbc33] It is arranged in three short sections, each devoted to one of the Three Jewels: recollection of the Buddha (*buddhānusmṛti*), of the Dharma (*dharmānusmṛti*) and of the Saṅgha (*saṃghānusmṛti*).[^dsbc33]

The text is made up of formulaic epithets. The first section begins with the familiar list of the Buddha's titles (*bhagavān*, *tathāgata*, *arhat*, *samyaksaṃbuddha* and so on) and continues with a series of praises of his qualities. The second describes the Dharma as well taught, good in the beginning, middle and end, and leading to awakening, and the third calls the noble Saṅgha well practising and a field of merit for the world, before the colophon closes the sūtra.[^dsbc33]

Little reliable information on this work could be found: the digital edition names no printed source, author, date or place of composition, and the sources consulted did not document the text's history, manuscripts or translations. Nothing is therefore asserted here about those matters.
