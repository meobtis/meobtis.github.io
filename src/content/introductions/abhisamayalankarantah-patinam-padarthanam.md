This short Sanskrit text is headed "appendix 1" (*pariśiṣṭam*) and opens with a salutation to all buddhas and bodhisattvas. Its title means roughly "the topics contained in the Ornament of Clear Realization", and it gives a brief explanation of the topics of the *Abhisamayālaṅkāra*. It is not an independent ancient work: the DSBC record names it as part of Ram Shankar Tripathi's 1977 edition published at Sarnath, and the text itself states that it presents in summary the eight topics (*padārtha*) beginning with the knowledge of all aspects, together with the seventy topics beginning with the arousing of the thought of awakening.[^dsbc277]

The text sets out the eight topics, which it takes from two verses of the *Abhisamayālaṅkāra*: the knowledge of all aspects, path-knowledge, knowledge of the basis, the complete awakening to all aspects, the culminating realization, the serial realization, the instantaneous realization and the dharmakāya. It then treats each in turn, giving a definition, its divisions and its scope.[^dsbc277] This follows the standard scheme in which the first three topics are kinds of knowledge, the next four are applications of practice and the last is the result, with the seventy points distributed across the eight topics.[^lotsawa-seventy]

Little is documented about the appendix itself. The edition front matter does not name its author, and the sources consulted give no further information about it, so it is best regarded as a modern editorial aid to Haribhadra's commentary rather than a traditional work; this attribution is an inference from the edition record and not a documented fact.
