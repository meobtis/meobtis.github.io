# Satyasūtra

1.1 Satyasūtra  
🚹⨀①🆎(satya+sūtra)  
discourse on truth  
Satyasūtra

2.1 1 śrāvastyāṃ nidānam /  
🚺⨀⑦(śrāvastī) 🚻⨀①(nidāna)  
in Śrāvastī | setting  
The setting is in Śrāvastī.

2.2 2 ye kecid bhikṣavaḥ samyag eva śraddhayā agārād anagārikāṃ pravrajaṃti sarve te caturṇām ā*rya*satyānāṃ jñānāya /  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(yad) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(kaścit) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikṣu) ⏏️(samyak) ⏏️(eva) 🚺⨀③(śraddhā) 🚻⨀⑤(agāra) 🚺⨀②🆎(na+agārikā) 🟢⨂🤟▶️(pravrajati) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(sarva) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(tad) 🚹⨂⑥(catur) 🚻⨂⑥🆎(ārya+satya) 🚻⨀④(jñāna)  
who | any | bhikṣavaḥ | rightly | indeed | with confidence | from home | into homelessness | go forth | all | they | of the four | of the realisations | for knowledge  
Bhikṣavaḥ, whoever indeed rightly goes forth with `śraddhā` (confidence) from the household life into homelessness, all of them do so for the `jñāna` (knowledge) of the four `āryasatyas` (realisations).

2.3 3 katameṣāṃ caturṇām /  
*️⃣🤟🚻⨂⑥(katama) 🚹⨂⑥(catur)  
of which | of the four  
Of which four?

2.4 4 duḥkham āryasatyasya duḥkhasamudayasya duḥkhanirodhasya duḥkha*n*i*r*odhagāminī pratipad āryasatyasya jñānāya /  
🚻⨀①(duḥkha) 🚻⨀⑥🆎(ārya+satya) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(duḥkha+samudaya) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(duḥkha+nirodha) 🚺⨀①🆎(duḥkha+nirodha+gāmin) 🚺⨀①(pratipad) 🚻⨀⑥🆎(ārya+satya) 🚻⨀④(jñāna)  
dissatisfaction | of the realisation | of the origin of dissatisfaction | of the cessation of dissatisfaction | leading to the cessation of dissatisfaction | course of practice | of the realisation | for knowledge  
For the knowledge of the realisation of `duḥkha` (dissatisfaction), of the `samudaya` (origin) of dissatisfaction, of the `nirodha` (cessation) of dissatisfaction, and of the realisation of the path leading to the cessation of dissatisfaction.

2.5 5 tasmāt tarhi caturṇām āryasatyānām anabhisamitānām abhisamayāya atimātraṃ tīvra*ccha*ndo vyāyāmaś cotsuḍhiś cāprativāṇiś ca smṛtyā saṃprajanyena ca yogaḥ karaṇīyaḥ /  
⏏️(tasmāt) ⏏️(tarhi) 🚹⨂⑥(catur) 🚻⨂⑥🆎(ārya+satya) 🚻⨂⑥🔽(ta)🆎(na+abhisamita) 🚹⨀④(abhisamaya) ⏏️(atimātram) 🚹⨀①🆎(tīvra+chanda) 🚹⨀①(vyāyāma) ⏏️(ca) 🚺⨀①(utsuḍhi) ⏏️(ca) 🚺⨀①🆎(na+prativāṇi) ⏏️(ca) 🚺⨀③(smṛti) 🚻⨀③(saṃprajanya) ⏏️(ca) 🚹⨀①(yoga) 🚹⨀①🔽(anīya)(karaṇīya)  
therefore | then | of the four | of the realisations | of the uncomprehended | for comprehension | beyond measure | keen zeal | effort | and | perseverance | and | unflagging persistence | and | with awareness | with clear comprehension | and | application | to be made  
Therefore, then, for the comprehension of the four realisations not yet comprehended, exceedingly intense zeal, effort, perseverance, unflagging persistence, and application with `smṛti` (awareness) and clear comprehension must be made.

*(MS "abhisamayāya adhimātra tivraś ca vyāyāmaś" for "abhisamayāya adhimātraṃ tīvraś chandaś ca vyāyāmaś"?)*

3.2 7 duḥkham ā*rya*satyasya duḥkhasamudayasya *duḥkhanirodhasya* duḥkhanirodhagāminī pratipad *ārya*satyasya i*ti* /  
🚻⨀①(duḥkha) 🚻⨀⑥🆎(ārya+satya) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(duḥkha+samudaya) 🚹⨀⑥🆎(duḥkha+nirodha) 🚺⨀①🆎(duḥkha+nirodha+gāmin) 🚺⨀①(pratipad) 🚻⨀⑥🆎(ārya+satya) ⏏️(iti)  
dissatisfaction | of the realisation | of the origin of dissatisfaction | of the cessation of dissatisfaction | leading to the cessation of dissatisfaction | course of practice | of the realisation | thus  
Of the realisation of dissatisfaction, of the origin of dissatisfaction, of the cessation of dissatisfaction, and of the realisation of the path leading to the cessation of dissatisfaction—thus.
