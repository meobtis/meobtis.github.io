The *Nāmāṣṭaśatakaya* is a short Sanskrit hymn (*stotra*) addressed to Śākyamuni Buddha. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon lists it among the stotra of the śāstrapiṭaka, under the heading for Śākyamuni, as a single-chapter text.[^dsbc178] The title suggests a hymn of 108 names (*nāmāṣṭaśataka*), although this is an inference from the title, not a statement in the sources consulted.

The text opens with the salutation *namaḥ śākyasiṃhāya* ("homage to the Lion of the Śākyas") and then proceeds in numbered verses, each addressing the Buddha with a string of epithets, such as "lotus-eyed" and "omniscient", and closing with the refrain "I bow to you".[^dsbc178] Little reliable information about its author, date, place of composition or any Tibetan or Chinese translation could be found, and the digital edition's record does not name a printed source, editor or publisher.[^dsbc178]
