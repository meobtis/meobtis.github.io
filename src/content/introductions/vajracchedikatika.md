The *Vajracchedikāṭīkā* is a commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā*, the Diamond Sūtra. The heading of the digital text describes it as an extended commentary (*vistṛtaṭīkā*) on the sūtra, composed by the teacher (*ācārya*) Kamalaśīla; the work opens with a salutation to Mañjuśrī and verses of homage.[^gretil-vajratika]

Little reliable information about the work could be found. The Kamalaśīla named in the heading is presumably the Indian Buddhist master (c. 740–795 CE) who travelled to Tibet with Śāntarakṣita and is known for the *Bhāvanākrama* ("Stages of Meditation") and a commentary on the *Madhyamālaṃkāra*; a general reference on him does not mention a Diamond Sūtra commentary, so the attribution, the date and the work's relation to any Tibetan translation could not be confirmed here.[^wikipedia-kamalashila] The printed edition behind the digital text was prepared by Pema Tendzin and published at Sarnath in 1994.[^gretil-vajratika]
