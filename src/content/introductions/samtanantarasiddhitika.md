The *Saṃtānāntarasiddhiṭīkā* (*Santānāntarasiddhiṭīkā*) is a Sanskrit commentary attributed to Vinītadeva on the *Saṃtānāntarasiddhi*, a short treatise by Dharmakīrti usually rendered "Proof of Other Mindstreams" or "Proof of Other Minds".[^wiki-dharmakirti] The digital texts open with the root verse, which says that one infers another mind from purposeful action observed in one's own body and in others, and the commentary then explains it.[^gretil-stk] The English title used here is descriptive, as no established English name for the commentary was found.

Dharmakīrti's treatise is described in reference works as one of his two minor works and as an argument that the idealist (Yogācāra) can use the same argument from analogy for other minds as the realist, and so avoid solipsism.[^tillemans2026] The commentary's opening presents the opponent's objection that, if only mind exists, perception, inference and scripture cannot establish other beings, and then answers it by appeal to inference.[^gretil-stk] Little else about the commentary, including its precise date and any Tibetan or Chinese translation, could be verified in the sources consulted, and it is left undiscussed here rather than guessed.
