The *Sambandhaparīkṣā* ("Examination of Relations", literally an examination of *sambandha*, "relation" or "connection") is a short treatise by the Buddhist logician Dharmakīrti. It is a verse text with an accompanying prose commentary, the *Sambandhaparīkṣāvṛtti*.[^steinkellner2022]

Its subject is the refutation of the various kinds of relation, including causality, that Indian philosophical schools held to exist in reality. Dharmakīrti's position is that such relations are only conceptually constructed.[^steinkellner2022]

Authorship of the commentary is a matter of record rather than certainty. Colophons of the Tibetan translations in the Tanjur name Dharmakīrti as its author, but a Sanskrit manuscript from Drepung monastery names Devendrabuddhi, who is traditionally regarded as Dharmakīrti's pupil.[^steinkellner2022]

Both the verses and the commentary survive in Sanskrit and in Tibetan. Steinkellner's 2022 critical edition, in the series Sanskrit Texts from the Tibetan Autonomous Region, is based on the Drepung manuscript and also re-edits the two Tibetan translations. It was prompted by the discovery of that manuscript and by Yaita's identification of the last three verses in Sanskrit.[^steinkellner2022]
