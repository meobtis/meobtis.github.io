The Śrāvakabhūmi ("Stage of the Disciples" or "Levels of the Hearers") is one of the books of the *Yogācārabhūmi*, the large compendium of Buddhist yoga practice and doctrine. It is the tenth of the fourteen books of the Basic Section (the *Bhūmivastu*) and, after the *Bodhisattvabhūmi*, is described as the second largest book of that work; it is organised in four *yogasthāna*s (sections on practice).[^wiki-ybh]

As its name indicates, the book deals with the path of the *śrāvaka*, the "hearer" or disciple, rather than with the bodhisattva path treated in the *Bodhisattvabhūmi*. The opening passage of the Sanskrit text begins with the "basis of the lineage" (*gotrabhūmi*), and it goes on to discuss the conditions needed for practice, such as being born as a human in a central region, having intact faculties and faith in the Buddha's teaching, and the favourable circumstances provided by others.[^gretil-shukla]

Like the rest of the *Yogācārabhūmi*, the book is traditionally attributed to Asaṅga, the name given in the GRETIL and DSBC records of this text.[^gretil-shukla][^dsbc336] Several modern scholars, among them Schmithausen, Aramaki, Deleanu and Wayman, regard the Śrāvakabhūmi as belonging to the oldest layer of the *Yogācārabhūmi*, though the question of the work's composition remains a matter of scholarly discussion.[^wiki-ybh] Most of the Basic Section, including this book, survives in Sanskrit, and complete Tibetan and Chinese translations of the whole *Yogācārabhūmi* exist, the latter attributed to Xuanzang.[^wiki-ybh]

Two printed Sanskrit editions underlie the digital texts: Karunesha Shukla's 1973 Patna edition and the revised text prepared by the Śrāvakabhūmi Study Group of Taishō University in Tokyo, which gives the Sanskrit alongside a Japanese translation.[^gretil-shukla][^gretil-tokyo] Detailed information on the manuscript, the Tibetan and Chinese catalogue numbers and the English translations of this book could not be verified in the sources consulted and is therefore not given here.
