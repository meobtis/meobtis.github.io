The *Śrī Vajravārāhisādhana* ("Sādhana of the glorious Vajravārāhī") is a short Sanskrit work in verse, with interspersed prose explanations and mantras, that gives a stepwise meditation practice (*sādhana*) centred on the Buddhist goddess Vajravārāhī. In the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon it is filed under the commentarial literature on the Yoginī tantras (*yoginītantraṭīkā*), within the *śāstrapiṭaka* section.[^dsbc351]

From the text itself, the practice opens with a salutation to Vajrayoginī and a promise to set out the sādhana "as taught", addressed to a vocative "Vikramasena". It then proceeds through the sevenfold offering, the four immeasurables (loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy and equanimity), a meditation on emptiness with mantras, the visualisation of a protective vajra enclosure with guardian goddesses and kīla-pinning mantras, and the visualisation of the seed syllable *vaṃ* in a cremation-ground setting.[^dsbc351]

The edition records supply no information on authorship, date, manuscript sources or translations, and the available records name no editor or printed edition. Beyond what the text itself shows, little reliable information could be gathered, so questions of attribution and transmission are left open here.[^dsbc351]
