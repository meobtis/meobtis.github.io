The *Abhayapaddhati* is a Sanskrit commentary by Abhayākaragupta, an influential Indian Buddhist master of the early second millennium, on the *Buddhakapālatantra*, a scripture of the yoginītantra class, which tantric Buddhists regarded as the highest category of esoteric scriptures.[^oeaw] No established English title for the commentary appears in the sources consulted, so it is usually cited by its Sanskrit name.

The text on this site begins with chapter 9 (*caryāpaṭala*) and consists of short glosses that quote a word or phrase of the root tantra and explain it. Little else about the work is documented in the sources consulted here, and no information on its date, manuscripts or Tibetan and Chinese translations could be verified. Its edition was published in the series Sanskrit Texts from the Tibetan Autonomous Region, which makes available Sanskrit manuscripts preserved in Tibet.[^oeaw] The volume's publication year is given as 2013 by the publisher and 2012 by a distributor's record.[^oeaw][^austriaca]
