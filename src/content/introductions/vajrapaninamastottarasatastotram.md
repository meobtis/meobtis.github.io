The *Vajrapāṇināmāṣṭottaraśatastotram* ("hymn of the one hundred and eight names of Vajrapāṇi") is a short Sanskrit stotra, a poem of praise. It is arranged in numbered verses, each of which strings together epithets of the deity and closes with a salutation of the form "homage to you" (*namo 'stu te*). The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon (DSBC) files it under the stotra literature devoted to Vajrapāṇi.[^dsbc205]

The opening verses address the deity as Vajrasattva and as the great being (*mahāsattva*), and go on to "vajra" and "mahā" compounds such as *vajrarāja*, *vajrarāga* and *vajrasādhu*.[^dsbc205] This is the usual manner of a nāmāṣṭottaraśata, a litany in which each name is a short compound and the sequence of epithets is itself the object of devotion.

Little reliable information about the text's authorship, date, provenance or any Tibetan or Chinese translation could be found in the sources consulted, and none is asserted here. The only printed edition identified is the one named in the DSBC record, the *Bauddha Stotra Saṃgraha* edited by Pandey (1994).[^dsbc205]
