The *Pramāṇavārttikasvavṛtti* ("Autocommentary on the Commentary on Epistemology") is the prose commentary that the Buddhist logician Dharmakīrti wrote on his own verse treatise, the *Pramāṇavārttika*.[^tillemans2026] It is conventionally cited as SV. As edited by Gnoli, it covers the first chapter of the *Pramāṇavārttika*, which deals with inference.[^tillemans2026][^gretil-sv]

Little else about the work could be verified for this introduction. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy lists Gnoli's 1960 edition as the edition of the Sanskrit of the autocommentary, and notes that Frauwallner's hypothesis that a proto-text later became the autocommentary is still speculative.[^tillemans2026] The same source says that scholars still debate whether Dharmakīrti lived in the sixth or the seventh century CE, with Frauwallner proposing 600–660, Krasser pushing his activity back to the mid-sixth century, and Balcerowicz proposing 550–610.[^tillemans2026] Its account of the translations and commentarial reception of the autocommentary was not consulted in detail, so no Tibetan or Chinese translation history is given here.

The digital text is the Sanskrit of Gnoli's edition, keyed for a word-indexed database in which the position of each word is recorded by a numeric code referring to page and line of the printed text.[^gretil-sv]
