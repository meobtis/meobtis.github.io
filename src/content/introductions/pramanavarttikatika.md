The *Pramāṇavārttikaṭīkā* is a commentary on Dharmakīrti's *Pramāṇavārttika*, the verse treatise on epistemology and logic, by Śākyabuddhi. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy describes Śākyabuddhi as an eighth-century commentator and names him, with Devendrabuddhi, among the Indian commentators on Dharmakīrti.[^tillemans2026] The text in this edition is not a complete work but transcribed manuscript leaves, beginning and ending with gaps, and its commentary glosses verses word by word on points such as universals (*sāmānya*), conventional signs and the particular (*svalakṣaṇa*).[^gretil-zakya]

Little more could be verified from the sources consulted for this introduction. The Stanford Encyclopedia notes that Śākyabuddhi's comment on verse I.216 treats scripture as needed by those pursuing the path but not grounded in objective fact, and that selections from his and Devendrabuddhi's commentaries have been translated in English alongside parts of the *Pramāṇavārttika*.[^tillemans2026] Its Tibetan translation, further manuscript history and reception are not described here.
