The *Upodghātaḥ* ("introduction") is a short Sanskrit text that, according to the bibliographic record of the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon, is the introduction to a *Caryāgītikoṣa*, published by Visva Bharati at Santiniketan in 1956; no editor is named in the record.[^dsbc353] No established English title could be verified from the sources consulted. The Canon files the text under commentarial literature (*ṭīkā*) on yoginī tantras.[^dsbc353]

Little further reliable information could be gathered for this introduction. The text itself opens with a sequence of numbered verses of homage, headed for example "homage to the sahaja", "homage to the twilight language" (*saṃdhyābhāṣā*) and "homage to the eighty-four siddhas", and goes on to quote verses on the *saṃdhyābhāṣā* and on the families of the tantric consorts.[^dsbc353] Its relation to the *Caryāgīti* songs, the authorship of the introduction and its date, and the question of any Tibetan or Chinese counterparts are not documented in the sources consulted, so they are not discussed here; the reader should consult specialist scholarship on the *Caryāgīti* literature.
