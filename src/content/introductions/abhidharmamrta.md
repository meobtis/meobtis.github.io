The *Abhidharmāmṛta* ("Nectar of Abhidharma") is an Abhidharma treatise whose text opens by naming the "venerable Ghoṣaka" as its author. In the digital text it is divided into fifteen sections, called *bindu* ("drops"), beginning with giving (*dāna*) and moral conduct (*śīla*).[^dsbc97] Library catalogues describe it as a treatise on the doctrine of salvation and on psychology from the Sarvāstivāda point of view.[^nii1953][^openlibrary1953]

The Sanskrit text now in circulation is not a transmitted manuscript. The 1953 Visva-Bharati publication is described as translated from Chinese with notes and an introductory study, and the Chinese work is catalogued as 阿毘曇甘露味論, attributed to Ghoṣa (瞿沙).[^nii1953] Little else about the work's date, author and transmission could be verified in the sources consulted here, so these matters are left open. No established English title was found.
