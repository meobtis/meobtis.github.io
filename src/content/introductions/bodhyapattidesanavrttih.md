The *Bodhyāpattideśanāvṛtti* ("Commentary on the Confession of Bodhisattva Transgressions") is a short Sanskrit prose commentary on a sūtra of confession. The text opens with salutations to Mañjuśrī and to the Buddha Śākyamuni and a verse stating the author's aim of explaining the "confession of bodhisattva offences", which purifies the transgressions of a "son of the Jina".[^dsbc262] The commentary identifies its root text as the Triskandha-sūtra ("Three Heaps"), says that it belongs to the sūtra division of the three baskets and the twelve branches of the teaching, and also describes it as mainly a Mahāyāna Vinaya text, since it treats both the basis and the members of an offence.[^dsbc262]

As far as the text itself shows, its exposition covers the purpose and subject matter of the sūtra: the offences of beginner bodhisattvas and the means of purifying them. It explains that purification works through four powers and repeated confession, including a rite of prostration before the thirty-five buddhas. It also gives two traditions about the occasion of the teaching, one in which Upāli asks the Buddha to teach a confession for thirty-five novice bodhisattvas at Rājagṛha.[^dsbc262] It addresses the objection that the sūtra lacks the usual opening and closing formulae, replying that the passage is drawn from the Ratnakūṭa collection.[^dsbc262]

The DSBC records give no author, date, printed edition or manuscript for this work, and the front matter of both digital versions leaves the bibliographic fields (title, editor, publisher, place and year) empty.[^dsbc262][^dsbc556] I could not find independent scholarly sources describing this commentary, so its authorship, date, and any Tibetan or Chinese translation remain undocumented here. No established English title is known to me.
