The *Tattvasiddhi* ("Establishment of Reality") is a short Sanskrit work on Vajrayāna Buddhism. The text opens with homage to Vajrasattva, then states that the author, having bowed to the Vajrayāna, will explain the *Tattvasiddhi* in order to dispel delusion. The prose that follows addresses practitioners whose good roots are immature, who lack a teacher's guidance and who have not understood the meaning of the path.[^dsbc359]

The heading of the digital text attributes the work to the teacher Śāntarakṣita.[^dsbc359] An encyclopaedic survey of his works lists a *Tattvasiddhi* in the Tibetan canon (Derge 39a1, Peking 42a8) and describes it as a philosophical defence of tantra, while noting that the authorship is doubtful.[^wiki-santaraksita] Little else about its date, sources or transmission is documented in the sources consulted here, and the attribution should be treated as unverified.
