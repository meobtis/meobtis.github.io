The *Tarkabhāṣāṭīkā* is a Sanskrit commentary on the *Tarkabhāṣā*, a work on Buddhist epistemology (*pramāṇa*) and logic. The digital edition is catalogued under the title "Śrīmokṣākaraguptaviracitā tarkabhāṣāṭīkā", which associates the work with Mokṣākaragupta, the author to whom the *Tarkabhāṣā* itself is traditionally attributed.[^dsbc323] The opening of the text indicates that it comments on the *Tarkabhāṣā* passage by passage, beginning with the definition of a means of valid cognition (*pramāṇa*) as correct cognition of an object not previously known.

The text was printed in Baroda in 1942 by The Oriental Institute in an edition prepared by Beynotosh Bhattacharyya.[^dsbc323][^dsbc470] Little else about the work, including its precise authorship, date and manuscript sources, could be verified in sources consulted for this entry, and these points are left open here.
