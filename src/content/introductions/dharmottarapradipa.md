The *Dharmottarapradīpa* ("Lamp on Dharmottara") is a Sanskrit subcommentary by Durvekamiśra (Durveka Miśra) on Dharmottara's *Nyāyabinduṭīkā*, which is in turn a commentary on Dharmakīrti's *Nyāyabindu*.[^gretil-dp] The *Nyāyabindu*, often rendered "Drop of Reasoning", is one of Dharmakīrti's works on epistemology and logic.[^sep-dharmakirti] Dharmottara's commentary is the only one of his works known to survive in the original Sanskrit.[^wiki-dharmottara] The *Pradīpa* follows the *Ṭīkā* closely: in the digital edition the opening words of the *Ṭīkā* passages being explained are set in bold.[^gretil-dp]

Little reliable information about the author and the work could be found in the sources consulted. The GRETIL header dates the text to about 1100, but this is an editorial note and was not checked against other scholarship here.[^gretil-dp] The printed text used for both digital editions is Dalsukhbhai Malvania's 1971 edition in the Tibetan Sanskrit Works series, published in Patna.[^gretil-dp][^sarit-dp] The GRETIL header also records several torn or missing leaves in the underlying material.[^gretil-dp]
