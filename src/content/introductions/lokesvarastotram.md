The *Lokeśvarastotram* is a short Sanskrit hymn of praise (*stotra*) addressed to Lokeśvara, "Lord of the World". The text calls its subject Śrīlokanātha, a name of the bodhisattva of compassion Avalokiteśvara, under which the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files it.[^dsbc128] It has eleven verses, in which each of the first ten ends with the same refrain, "I worship the blessed Lokanātha, the wise, the giver of good", and the last verse promises that Lokanātha will quickly grant the wishes of those who recite the hymn.[^dsbc128] The verses praise his form and ornaments, his compassion, and his power to relieve fear, misfortune and famine.[^dsbc128]

Little reliable information on the hymn's author, date or place of composition could be found, and no Tibetan or Chinese translation or manuscript tradition has been verified here, so none is asserted. The digital texts record only that the Romanized version derives from Pandey's printed collection of Buddhist hymns.[^dsbc128] Its relation to other stotras of Avalokiteśvara or Lokanātha in that collection is not documented in the sources consulted.
