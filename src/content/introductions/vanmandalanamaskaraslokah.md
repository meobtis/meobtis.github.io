The *Vāṅmaṇḍalanamaskāraślokāḥ* ("verses of homage to the speech mandala") is a short Sanskrit verse text. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon (DSBC) lists it among the Advaya tantra commentarial works of its śāstrapiṭaka division.[^dsbc745] It consists of a single section of salutation verses, and no established English title could be found.

From the verses themselves, the text pays homage to a circle of goddesses: it begins with a one-faced, three-eyed deity and names Carcikā in the eastern position, standing on a corpse and seated on a red lotus, with further verses describing eight surrounding goddesses on lotus petals.[^dsbc745] Little else about the work is documented in the sources consulted. Its authorship, date, tradition and any Tibetan or Chinese translation could not be verified, and are therefore left unstated here.
