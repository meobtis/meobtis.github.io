The *Vādanyāyaṭīkā*, also called *Vipañcitārthā*, is a commentary by Śāntarakṣita on Dharmakīrti's *Vādanyāya* ("Logic of Argumentation"). The editions here present the root text with the commentary, and the opening verses invoke Mañjuśrī and announce a concise exposition of the divisions of the logic of debate.[^dsbc282]

Dharmakīrti's *Vādanyāya* deals with the conduct of debate. Tillemans reports that, according to Frauwallner's ordering, it is the last of Dharmakīrti's five treatises, and that in it Dharmakīrti takes a stricter line on inference for others, treating the statement of the thesis as a point of defeat (*nigrahasthāna*).[^tillemans2026] Beyond this, little reliable information on the commentary itself (its date, translations or manuscripts) was found in the sources consulted; the attribution to Śāntarakṣita is that given by the editions' own title pages.[^sarit-vadanyayatika]
