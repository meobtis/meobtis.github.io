The *Āryaprajñāpāramitāvajracchedikāṭīkā* is a commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā*, the "Diamond Sūtra". In the digital text, a heading describes it as an extended commentary (*vistṛtaṭīkā*) on the sūtra composed by the teacher Kamalaśīla, and the work opens with a salutation to Mañjuśrī and verses of homage and intention.[^dsbc279]

Beyond this front matter, little reliable information could be found on the work. The Kamalaśīla named in the heading is presumably the Indian Buddhist master of roughly 740–795 CE who taught in Tibet, known for his *Stages of Meditation* and a commentary on the *Madhyamālaṃkāra*; a general reference on him does not mention a commentary on the Diamond Sūtra, so the attribution, its relation to Tibetan canonical translations and the date of the work could not be confirmed here.[^wikipedia-kamalashila] The printed edition on which the digital text rests was prepared by Pema Tendzin and published at Sarnath in 1994.[^dsbc279]
