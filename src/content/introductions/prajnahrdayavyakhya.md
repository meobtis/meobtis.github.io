The *Prajñāhṛdayavyākhyā* ("Explanation of the Heart [of the Perfection of Wisdom]") is a short Sanskrit prose commentary on the Heart Sūtra (*Prajñāpāramitāhṛdaya*). It is held in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon among the *prajñāpāramitā* treatises of the *śāstrapiṭaka*, in Romanized and Devanagari forms.[^dsbc904][^dsbc905] The records name no author, editor or printed source, and no independent scholarly discussion of the work could be consulted in preparing this note, so its authorship, date and transmission remain undocumented here.

The text itself opens with a salutation to the Perfection of Wisdom and then sets out an introductory framework for reading the sūtra: it distinguishes the "introduction" (*upodghāta*) from the "occasion" (*nidāna*) of the sūtra, and refers to the views of the ācāryas Vimalamitra and Dignāga on how the sūtra's meaning is to be explained.[^dsbc904] It also cites verses attributed in the text to Dignāga (from a work it names as *Prajñāpāramitāpiṇḍārtha*) and discusses the setting, the assembly and the questions put to Avalokiteśvara. The DSBC records give no further information about the work's place in the commentarial literature.[^dsbc904]
