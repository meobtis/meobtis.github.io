The *Mahāsāṃghika Prātimokṣa Sūtra* is the Sanskrit recension of the code of monastic rules (*prātimokṣa*) belonging to the Mahāsāṃghika order. In Buddhist monastic practice a prātimokṣa is the list of training rules recited by the assembled community of monks, and several such codes, belonging to different ordination lineages, survive.[^wiki-pratimoksa] The text opens with a short verse section in praise of the Buddha and the recitation of the rules, followed by the rules themselves.[^dsbc90]

The Mahāsāṃghika school's complete Vinaya survives in Chinese translation as the *Mohesengzhi lü* (Taishō no. 1425), which, according to the account summarised in a general reference article, was brought to China by Faxian and translated in 416 CE.[^wiki-mahasamghika] Charles S. Prebish's monograph on the Sanskrit prātimokṣa sūtras of the Mahāsāṃghikas and Mūlasarvāstivādins is the standard study listed for this literature.[^wiki-pratimoksa] Little else about the manuscript basis and the date of this particular Sanskrit text could be verified from the sources consulted here, and readers should consult the introduction to the 1956 edition and Prebish's work for detail.
