The *Bauddha tantra kośa* ("Dictionary of Buddhist Tantra") is a Sanskrit-language reference work on Buddhist tantric literature. The digital text is Part I of a multi-part work edited by Vajravallabha Dwivedi and Thinlay Ram Shashni and issued by the Central Institute of Higher Tibetan Studies at Sarnath in 1990.[^dsbc229] No established English title was found; "Buddhist Tantra Kosha" is the form used by a bookseller listing a later volume.[^exotic]

From the text itself, the work is organised as an alphabetical series of headwords in Sanskrit (the first entries are *akaraṇasaṃvaraḥ*, *akāraḥ*, *akṣaram* and *akṣobhyam*). Under each headword the compilers quote relevant passages from tantric and related works, each followed by an abbreviated source reference and page number, such as "va. ti. ṭī., pṛ. 72".[^dsbc229] It thus appears to function as a concordance of definitions and usages drawn from the literature, rather than as a continuous treatise.

Little else is documented in the sources that could be found. The record of a later volume gives the same institute as publisher, Thinley Ram Shashni as author, a date of 2007, 344 pages, and describes it as Sanskrit only, which shows that the dictionary continued in further volumes; the listing offers no account of the scope or the number of volumes.[^exotic] Details of the sources abbreviated in the entries, and of the compilers' method, were not verified here.
