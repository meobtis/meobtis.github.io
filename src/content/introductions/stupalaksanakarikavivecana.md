The *Stūpalakṣaṇakārikāvivecana* ("Commentary on the verses on the characteristics of the stūpa") is a short Sanskrit work on the form and proportions of the Buddhist stūpa. The digital text opens with verses stating that the characteristics of the stūpa, taught only sparingly and in scattered places in the *prakīrṇaka* section of the Vinaya of the Lokottaravādins and in the sūtras of other schools, are here set out together; the edition's heading attributes the work to the Ācārya Bhadravyūha of the Ārya-Lokottaravāda, and it also includes a related passage titled *prakīrṇaka-caitya-lakṣaṇa*.[^gretil-slkv]

Beyond the edition's own front matter and the text itself, little reliable documentation on this work could be consulted in preparing this introduction. Its date, the identity of Bhadravyūha and the manuscript on which the edition rests are therefore not discussed here; readers should consult Roth's edition and the volume in which it was reprinted for these matters.[^gretil-slkv]
