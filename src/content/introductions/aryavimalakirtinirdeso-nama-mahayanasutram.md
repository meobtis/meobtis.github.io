The *Āryavimalakīrtinirdeśa* is a Mahāyāna sūtra known in English as "The Teaching of Vimalakīrti". The digital text is divided into twelve chapters, beginning with the "introduction on the purification of buddha-fields" (*buddhakṣetrapariśuddhinidāna*).[^dsbc35]

The text opens in the usual way ("Thus have I heard"), with the Buddha dwelling at Vaiśālī in the Āmrapālī grove among a large assembly of monks and bodhisattvas. The Licchavi youth Ratnākara, with five hundred companions, offers jewelled parasols; the Buddha merges them into one, and Ratnākara then asks how a bodhisattva's buddha-field is purified. The Buddha's reply begins the teaching, stating that the beings to be trained are the bodhisattva's buddha-field.[^dsbc35]

Little further can be stated here on the sūtra's date, authorship, translations or manuscripts, since no reliable external sources could be consulted for this entry; readers should consult scholarly introductions and translations of the Vimalakīrtinirdeśa for these matters.
