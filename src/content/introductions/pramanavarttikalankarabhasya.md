The *Pramāṇavārttikālaṅkārabhāṣya* is a Sanskrit commentary by Prajñākaragupta on the *Pramāṇavārttika* of Dharmakīrti, the central work of Buddhist epistemology and logic. It is also known as the *Pramāṇavārttikabhāṣya* or the *Vārttikālaṅkāra*, and is counted among the principal commentaries on Dharmakīrti's text.[^sep-dharmakirti][^sarit-pvab] The digital text begins with an invocation and a verse announcing that the establishment of the means of valid cognition (*pramāṇasiddhi*) will be set out in due order, and it follows the chapter structure of the root work, beginning with the chapter on *pramāṇasiddhi*.[^sarit-pvab]

Little reliable information on the work's date, transmission and reception could be verified for this introduction. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy notes only that Prajñākaragupta is a commentator on the *Pramāṇavārttika*, and that part of the second chapter (II.34–72) with his commentary has been translated into English by Franco (1997).[^sep-dharmakirti] The digital edition is based on a facsimile of Sanskrit manuscripts of the work published in 1998, and names Rāhula Sāṅkṛtyāyana as editor.[^sarit-pvab]
