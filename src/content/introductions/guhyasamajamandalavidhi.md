The Guhyasamājamaṇḍalavidhi ("Procedure for the Guhyasamāja Maṇḍala") is a short Sanskrit work in verse. It opens with a salutation to Vajrasattva and a verse of homage to Mañjuśrī, then sets out the qualifications of the officiating teacher, who must be initiated into the Guhyasamāja cycle, and goes on to the preparation and drawing of the maṇḍala.[^gretil-gmv] The two digital editions share this opening; they differ in readings and verse numbering. The GRETIL edition names the author as Dīpaṃkarabhadra, whereas the DSBC lists it without an author.[^dsbc357]

The Guhyasamāja Tantra is an important scripture of tantric Buddhism, called Unexcelled Yoga Tantra in Tibet; its exegesis is usually divided into an Ārya tradition, based on works attributed to Nāgārjuna, Āryadeva and Candrakīrti, and a Jñānapāda tradition, each with its own maṇḍala of deities.[^wiki-guhyasamaja] Which tradition this manual follows is not stated in the editions consulted here.

Little reliable information on this particular work, its author's date, or its Tibetan translation could be gathered in the research behind this page, so none is given. The details of its transmission are best sought in the Göttingen manuscript and the study published in Dhīḥ 42 (2006) that the GRETIL record cites.[^gretil-gmv]
