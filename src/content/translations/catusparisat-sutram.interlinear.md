# catuṣpariṣat sūtram (The Sūtra of the Four Assemblies)

1.1 catuṣpariṣat sūtram  
⏏️(catus) 🚺⨀①(pariṣad) 🚻⨀①(sūtra)  
four | assembly | discourse  
The Sūtra of the Four Assemblies

2.1 bodhisattvo bhagavān urubilvāyāṃ vihāraṃ nadyā nairañjanāyāstīre bodhimūle sātatyakārī nipako bodhipākṣikeṣu dharmeṣu bhāvanāyogam anuyuktoviharati |  
🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚺⨀⑦(urubilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihāra) 🚺⨀⑥(nadī) 🚺⨀⑥(nairañjanā) 🚻⨀⑦(tīra) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhi+mūla) 🚹⨀①🆎(sātatya+kārin) 🚹⨀①(nipaka) 🚹⨂⑦🆎(bodhi+pākṣika) 🚹⨂⑦(dharma) 🚺⨀①(bhāvanā) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+yoga) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati)  
bodhisattva | Bhagavān | in Urubilvā | dwelling | of the river | of Nairañjanā | on the bank | at the root of awakening | acting with perseverance | prudent | in those conducive to awakening | in qualities | cultivation | practice | devoted | dwells  
The bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Urubilvā on the bank of the river Nairañjanā at the root of `bodhi` (awakening), acting continuously and prudently, dwells devoted to the practice of cultivation in the qualities pertaining to awakening.

2.2 sa rātryāḥ prathame yāme ṛddhiviṣayajñānasākṣīkriyāyā abhijñāyāṃ cittaṃ abhinirnāmayatyanekavidham ṛddhiviṣayaṃ pratyanubhavati |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(prathama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚺⨀⑥🆎(ṛddhi+viṣaya+jñāna+sākṣi+kriyā) 🚺⨀⑦(abhijñā) 🚻⨀②(citta) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+bhi+nirnāma+yati+na+eka+vidha) 🚹⨀②🆎(ṛddhi+viṣaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pratyanubhavati)  
he | of the night | in the first | in the watch | of realisation of knowledge of the range of psychic power | in experiential understanding | mind | directs, manifold | range of psychic power | experiences  
In the first watch of the night, he directs his `citta` (mind) to the `abhijñā` (experiential understanding) of the direct realisation of the `jñāna` (knowledge) of the domain of extraordinary abilities, and he experiences the manifold domain of extraordinary abilities.

2.3 tadyathā eko bhūtvā bahudhā bhavati |bahudhā bhūtvā eko bhavati |āvirbhavati tirobhāvaṃ jñānadarśanena pratyanubhavati |  
⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(eka) 🔽(tvā)(bhavati) ⏏️(bahudhā) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(bhavati) ⏏️(bahudhā) 🔽(tvā)(bhavati) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(eka) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(bhavati) 🚹⨀⑦🆎(āvi+bhavat) 🚹⨀②🆎(tiras+bhāva) 🚻⨀③🆎(jñāna+darśana) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pratyanubhavati)  
namely | one | having been | manifold | becomes | manifold | having been | one | becomes | appears | disappearance | by knowledge and vision | experiences  
Namely, having been one, he becomes many; having been many, he becomes one; he becomes manifest, and through knowledge and vision he experiences disappearance.

2.4 tiraskuḍyaṃ tiraḥśailaṃ tiraḥprākāram asajyamānaḥ kāyena gacchati tadyathā ākāśe pṛthivyām unmajjananimajjanaṃ karoti tadyathā udake|  
🚻⨀②🆎(tiras+kuḍya) 🚹⨀②🆎(tir+śaila) 🚹⨀②🆎(tir+prākāra) 🚹⨀①🔽(māna)🆎(na+sajyamān) 🚹⨀③(kāya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(gacchati) ⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) 🚹⨀⑦(ākāśa) 🚺⨀⑦(pṛthivī) 🚹⨀①🔽(at)(unmajjat) 🚻⨀②🆎(na+nimajjana) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(karoti) ⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) 🚻⨀⑦(udaka)  
through a wall | through a mountain | through a rampart | unobstructed | with the body | goes | just as | in space | in the earth | emerging | submerging | performs | just as | in water  
Through walls, through rocks, and through ramparts he goes unhindered with his body, just as in `ākāśa` (space); he rises up and sinks down in the earth just as in water.

2.5 udake asajyamānaḥ kāyena gacchati tadyathā pṛthivyām |  
🚻⨀⑦(udaka) 🚹⨀①🔽(māna)🆎(na+sajyamān) 🚹⨀③(kāya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(gacchati) ⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) 🚺⨀⑦(pṛthivī)  
on water | without sinking | with the body | goes | just as | on the earth  
He goes unhindered on water with his body just as on the earth.

2.6 ākāśe paryaṃgena vikramate tadyathā pakṣi śakuniḥ |  
🚹⨀⑦(ākāśa) 🟢⨀🤘⏹(paryaṅgati) 🚹⨀③(a) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(vikrāmati) ⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) 🚹⨀⓪(pakṣi) 🚹⨀①(śakuni)  
in space | cross-legged | in posture | travels | just as | winged | bird  
He moves cross-legged through ākāśa just as a winged bird.

2.7 imāvapi candrasūryau evaṃ mahardhikau evaṃ mahānubhāvau pāṇinā āmārjati parimārjayati yāvad brahmalokādapi kāyena vaśe vartayati |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨁②(idam) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨁②🆎(candra+sūrya) ⏏️(evam) ⏏️(mahar) 🚹⨁②🔽(ka)(dhika) ⏏️(evam) 🚹⨁②(mahānubhāva) 🚹⨀③(pāṇi) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(āmārjati) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(parimārjayati) ⏏️(yāvat) 🚹⨀⑤🆎(brahma+loka) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨀③(kāya) 🚹⨀⑦(vaśa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(vartayati)  
these two | even | moon and sun | so | great | of psychic power | so | of mahānubhāvau | with the hand | touches | strokes | as far as | from the world of Brahmā | even | with the body | under mastery | exercises  
Even these moon and sun, so greatly powerful, of such great might, he touches and strokes with his hand, and he exercises mastery with his body even up to the world of Brahmā.

2.8 iti bodhisattvo bhagavān uruvilvāyāṃ viharaṃ nadyā nairañjanāyāstīre bodhimūle sātatyakārī nipako bodhipākṣikeṣū dharmeṣu bhāvanāyogam anuyukto viharaṃ rātryāḥ prathame yāme anekavidham ṛddhiviṣayaṃ pratyanubhavati |  
⏏️(iti) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihara) 🚺⨀⑥(nadī) 🚺⨀⑥(nairañjanā) 🚻⨀⑦(tīra) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhi+mūla) 🚹⨀①🆎(sātatya+kārin) 🚹⨀①(nipaka) 🚹⨂①🆎(bodhi+pākṣika) 🚹⨁①(iṣu) 🚹⨂⑦(dharma) 🚺⨀①(bhāvanā) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+yoga) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🚹⨀②(vihara) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(prathama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+eka+vidha) 🚹⨀②🆎(ṛddhi+viṣaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pratyanubhavati)  
thus | bodhisattva | Bhagavān | in Uruvilvā | dwelling | of the river | of Nairañjanā | on the bank | at the root of awakening | acting with perseverance | prudent | conducive to awakening | among | in qualities | cultivation | practice | devoted | dwelling | of the night | in the first | in the watch | manifold | range of psychic power | experiences  
Thus the bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Uruvilvā on the bank of the river Nairañjanā at the root of awakening, acting continuously and prudently, dwelling devoted to the practice of cultivation in the qualities pertaining to awakening, experiences the manifold domain of extraordinary abilities in the first watch of the night.

2.9 atha bodhisattvo bhagavān uruvilvāyāṃ viharaṃ pūrvavad yāvad anuyukto viharati |  
⏏️(atha) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihara) ⏏️(pūrvavat) ⏏️(yāvat) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati)  
then | bodhisattva | Bhagavān | in Uruvilvā | dwelling | as before | as far as | devoted | dwells  
Then the bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Uruvilvā, as before, and so forth up to, dwells devoted.

2.10 sa rātryāḥ prathame yāme pūrvenivāsānusmṛtijñānasākṣīkriyāyā abhijñāyāṃ cittam abhinirnāmayati so'nekavidhaṃ pūrvenivāsaṃsamanusmarati |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(prathama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚺⨀⑥🆎(pūrve+nivāsa+anusmṛti+jñāna+sākṣi+kriyā) 🚺⨀⑦(abhijñā) 🚻⨀②(citta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(abhinirnamayati) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+eka+vidha) 🚹⨀⑦🔽(at)🆎(pūrva+nivā+samsamanusmarat)  
he | of the night | in the first | in the watch | of realisation of knowledge of recollection of former abodes | in experiential understanding | mind | directs | he | manifold | recollects former abode  
In the first watch of the night, he directs his citta to the abhijñā of the direct realisation of the knowledge of the recollection of past abodes, and he recollects manifold past abodes.

2.11 tadyathaikāṃ jātiṃ dve tisraścatasraḥ yāvad anekānapi saṃvartakalpān samanusmarati |  
⏏️🆎(tad+yathā) *️⃣🤟🚺⨀②(eka) 🚺⨀②(jāti) 🚺⨁②(dvi) 🚺⨂②(tri) 🚺⨂②(catur) ⏏️(yāvat) 🚹⨂②🆎(na+eka) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②🆎(saṃvarta+kalpa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(samanusmarati)  
namely | one | birth | two | three | four | up to | many | even | aeons of contraction | recollects  
Namely, he recollects one `jāti` (birth), two, three, four, and so forth up to even many aeons of contraction.

2.12 iti bodhisattvo bhagavāṃ uruvilvāyāṃ viharaṃ nadyā nairañjanāyāstīre bodhimūle sātatyakārī nipako bodhipākṣikeṣu dharmeṣu bhāvanāyogam anuyukto viharaṃ rātryāḥ prathame yāme pūrvenivāsaṃ samanusmarati |  
⏏️(iti) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) *️⃣🤟🚹⨁①🆎(bhaga+yuṣmad) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihara) 🚺⨀⑥(nadī) 🚺⨀⑥(nairañjanā) 🚻⨀⑦(tīra) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhi+mūla) 🚹⨀①🆎(sātatya+kārin) 🚹⨀①(nipaka) 🚹⨂⑦🆎(bodhi+pākṣika) 🚹⨂⑦(dharma) 🚺⨀①(bhāvanā) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+yoga) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🚹⨀②(vihara) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(prathama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚹⨀②🆎(pūrve+nivāsa) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(samanusmarati)  
thus | bodhisattva | Bhagavān | in Uruvilvā | dwelling | of the river | of Nairañjanā | on the bank | at the root of awakening | acting with perseverance | prudent | in those conducive to awakening | in qualities | cultivation | practice | devoted | dwelling | of the night | in the first | in the watch | former abode | recollects  
Thus the bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Uruvilvā on the bank of the river Nairañjanā at the root of awakening, acting continuously and prudently, dwelling devoted to the practice of cultivation in the qualities pertaining to awakening, recollects past abodes in the first watch of the night.

2.13 atha bodhisattvo bhagavān uruvilvāyāṃ viharaṃ pūrvavad yāvad anuyukto viharati |  
⏏️(atha) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihara) ⏏️(pūrvavat) ⏏️(yāvat) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati)  
then | bodhisattva | Bhagavān | in Uruvilvā | dwelling | as before | as far as | devoted | dwells  
Then the bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Uruvilvā, as before, and so forth up to, dwells devoted.

2.14 sa rātryā madhyame yāme divyaśrotrajñānasākṣīkriyāyā abhijñāyāṃ cittam abhinirnāmayati divyena śrotreṇa viśuddhenātikrāṃtamānuṣeṇaubhayām śṛṇoti mānuṣāṃśca ye vā dūre ye vāntike |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(madhyama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚺⨀⑥🆎(divya+śrotra+jñāna+sākṣi+kriyā) 🚺⨀⑦(abhijñā) 🚻⨀②(citta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(abhinirnamayati) 🚻⨀③(divya) 🚻⨀③(śrotra) 🚻⨀③(viśuddha) 🚻⨀③🆎(atikrānta+mānuṣa) 🚹⨀②🔽(ya)(aubhaya) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(śṛṇoti) 🚹⨂②(mānuṣa) ⏏️(ca) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(yad) ⏏️(vā) ⏏️(dūre) *️⃣🤟🚹⨂①(yad) ⏏️(vā) 🚻⨀⑦(antika)  
he | of the night | in the middle | in the watch | of realisation of knowledge of the divine ear | in experiential understanding | mind | directs | with the divine | with the ear | with the purified | with that surpassing the human | both | hears | human | and | those which | whether | far | those which | or | near  
In the middle watch of the night, he directs his citta to the abhijñā of the direct realisation of the knowledge of the celestial ear; with the purified celestial ear that surpasses the human, he hears both, and human ones, whether those far away or those nearby.

2.15 iti bodhisattvo bhagavān uruvilvāyāṃ viharaṃ nadyā nairañjanāyāstīre bodhimūle sātatyakārī nipako bodhipākṣikeṣu dharmeṣu bhāvanāyogam anuyukto viharaṃ rātryā madhyame yāme divyaśrotrajñānaṃ pratyanubhavati |  
⏏️(iti) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihara) 🚺⨀⑥(nadī) 🚺⨀⑥(nairañjanā) 🚻⨀⑦(tīra) 🚻⨀⑦🆎(bodhi+mūla) 🚹⨀①🆎(sātatya+kārin) 🚹⨀①(nipaka) 🚹⨂⑦🆎(bodhi+pākṣika) 🚹⨂⑦(dharma) 🚺⨀①(bhāvanā) 🚹⨀②🆎(na+yoga) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🚹⨀②(vihara) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(madhyama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚻⨂⑥🆎(divya+śrotra+jña) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(pratyanubhavati)  
thus | bodhisattva | Bhagavān | in Uruvilvā | dwelling | of the river | of Nairañjanā | on the bank | at the root of awakening | acting with perseverance | prudent | in those conducive to awakening | in qualities | cultivation | practice | devoted | dwelling | of the night | in the middle | in the watch | knowledge of the divine ear | experiences  
Thus the bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Uruvilvā on the bank of the river Nairañjanā at the root of awakening, acting continuously and prudently, dwelling devoted to the practice of cultivation in the qualities pertaining to awakening, experiences the knowledge of the celestial ear in the middle watch of the night.

2.16 atha bodhisattvo bhagavān uruvilvāyāṃ viharaṃ pūrvavad yāvad anuyukto viharati |  
⏏️(atha) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(bhagavat) 🚺⨀⑦(uruvilvā) 🚹⨀②(vihara) ⏏️(pūrvavat) ⏏️(yāvat) 🚹⨀①🔽(ta)(anuyukta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(viharati)

Then the bodhisattva Bhagavān, dwelling at Uruvilvā, as before, and so forth up to, dwells devoted.

2.17 sa rātryā madhyame yāme divyacakṣurjñānasākṣīkriyāyā vidyāyāṃ cittam abhinirnāmayati |  
*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚺⨀⑥(rātri) 🚹⨀⑦(madhyama) 🚹⨀⑦(yāma) 🚺⨀⑥🆎(divya+cakṣus+jñāna+sākṣi+kriyā) 🚺⨀⑦(vidyā) 🚻⨀②(citta) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(abhinirnamayati)  
he | of the night | in the middle | in the watch | of realisation of knowledge of the divine eye | in knowledge | mind | directs  
In the middle watch of the night, he directs his citta to the knowledge of the direct realisation of the knowledge of the celestial eye.

2.18 divyena cakṣuṣā viśuddhenātikrāntamānuṣeṇa sattvān paśyati cyavamānānapyupapadyamānānapi suvarṇānapi durvarṇānapi hīnānapi praṇītānapi sugatimapi gacchato durgatimapi yathākarmopagān sattvān yathābhūtaṃ prajānāti |  
🚻⨀③(divya) 🚻⨀③(cakṣus) 🚻⨀③(viśuddha) 🚻⨀③🆎(atikrānta+mānuṣa) 🚹⨂②(sattva) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati) 🚹⨂②🔽(māna)(cyavamāna) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②🔽(māna)(upapadyamāna) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②(suvarṇa) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②(durvarṇa) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②(hīna) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②(praṇīta) ⏏️(api) 🚺⨀②(sugati) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②🔽(at)(gacchat) 🚺⨀②(durgati) ⏏️(api) 🚹⨂②🆎(yathākarma+upaga) 🚹⨂②(sattva) ⏏️(yathābhūtam) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(prajānāti)  
with the divine | with the eye | with the purified | with that surpassing the human | beings | sees | passing away | both | re-arising | and | well-coloured | both | ill-coloured | and | inferior | both | superior | and | good destination | both | going | bad destination | and | faring according to action | beings | as it really is | understands  
With the purified celestial eye that surpasses the human, he sees beings passing away and rearising, fair and ugly, inferior and superior, going to a good destination and to a bad destination too; he understands as they really are beings faring according to their actions.
