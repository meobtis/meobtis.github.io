The *Triratnasaundaryagāthā* ("Verses on the Beauty of the Three Jewels") is a Sanskrit verse composition catalogued in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon under the *śāstrapiṭaka*, in its *kāvya* (poetic literature) section. The catalogue record names Sundarananda as editor and gives no publisher, place or year.[^dsbc811] The text is divided into numbered verses, with a running series of parenthetical numbers marking successive passages.

Judging from the opening verses of the digital text, the work begins with salutations to the guru and Gaṇeśa, praises the "three jewels" (*ratnatraya*), and then turns to the lineage of the Nepalese king Pṛthvīnārāyaṇa Śāha, describing his birth and his campaigns, including a battle at Kīrtipura.[^dsbc811] Little reliable information about the author, date, translations or printed edition could be found for this work, and no English title is established, so the description here rests only on the edition's own record and text.
