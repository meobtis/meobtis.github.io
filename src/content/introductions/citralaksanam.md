The *Citralakṣaṇa* ("Characteristics of Painting") is a short Sanskrit treatise on the art of painting. The text in this collection opens with salutations to Brahmā, Śiva, Viṣṇu and other deities and to Nagnajit, and announces that the author will set out the rules of painting in brief, drawing on earlier authorities.[^gretil-citra] A modern account describes the work, also known as the *Nagnajit-citralakṣaṇa*, as a painting treatise in three chapters attributed to Nagnajit, Viśvakarman and Prahlāda, whose first chapter contains a legend of the origin of painting.[^wiki-nagnajit]

Little reliable information about its date, authorship and transmission could be found. The same account reports that the original Sanskrit of the Tibetan version is considered lost, and that scholars have debated whether the Tibetan preserves only part of a larger work, whether several texts shared the title, or whether the attribution to Nagnajit is mistaken.[^wiki-nagnajit] This summary rests on a tertiary source and should be checked against specialist studies. The printed edition on which both digital texts are based presents the Sanskrit, Tibetan and English versions together.[^gretil-citra]
