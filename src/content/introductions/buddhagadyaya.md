*Buddhagadyaya* is a short Sanskrit text of praise addressed to the Buddha. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon (DSBC) files it under the stotra (hymn) division of its śāstra section, in a subcategory for Śākyamuni, and gives it as a single chapter.[^dsbc176] The romanised file opens with an alternative form of the title, "buddhagajjaya", followed by an homage to the Buddha (*namo buddhāya*) and numbered sections of short rhymed lines that salute the Buddha and celebrate his qualities, including repeated invocations beginning *jaya jaya dharmarāja*.[^dsbc176]

Little reliable information about the work could be found. The DSBC record leaves its bibliographic fields (title, editor, publisher, place and year) blank, so the printed edition on which the digital text rests is not identified, and no author, date, translation or manuscript tradition is documented in the sources consulted.[^dsbc176] The text is therefore best read as an anonymous devotional hymn of uncertain origin; its Devanagari counterpart is recorded separately in the same canon.[^dsbc693]
