# Satyasūtra

Satyasūtra

The setting is in Śrāvastī.  
Bhikṣavaḥ, whoever indeed rightly goes forth with `śraddhā` (confidence) from the household life into homelessness, all of them do so for the `jñāna` (knowledge) of the four `āryasatyas` (realisations).  
Of which four?  
For the knowledge of the realisation of `duḥkha` (dissatisfaction), of the `samudaya` (origin) of dissatisfaction, of the `nirodha` (cessation) of dissatisfaction, and of the realisation of the path leading to the cessation of dissatisfaction.  
Therefore, then, for the comprehension of the four realisations not yet comprehended, exceedingly intense zeal, effort, perseverance, unflagging persistence, and application with `smṛti` (awareness) and clear comprehension must be made.  
*(MS "abhisamayāya adhimātra tivraś ca vyāyāmaś" for "abhisamayāya adhimātraṃ tīvraś chandaś ca vyāyāmaś"?)*

Of which four?  
Of the realisation of dissatisfaction, of the origin of dissatisfaction, of the cessation of dissatisfaction, and of the realisation of the path leading to the cessation of dissatisfaction—thus.  
... The Satyasūtra is concluded.  
May there be protection. ... Hail.
