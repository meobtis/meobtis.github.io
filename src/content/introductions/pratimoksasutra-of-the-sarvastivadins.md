This text is a Sanskrit *Prātimokṣasūtra* of the Sarvāstivādins. The *prātimokṣa* is the compilation of rules of conduct that a community of Buddhist monks recites at its fortnightly assembly, and each Vinaya tradition transmitted its own version. The digital text opens with the introductory section (*nidāna*), which in the file is headed in German as "Einleitung", and it appears to preserve the manuscript's editorial markers, such as lacunae and uncertain readings.[^gretil-prmosu]

Little further is documented in the sources consulted for this entry. The GRETIL record names the printed basis as Georg von Simson's two-part edition, published in the series *Sanskrittexte aus den Turfanfunden*, which indicates that the text derives from manuscripts found at Turfan, but the record gives no description of the manuscripts, their date or their relation to the Chinese and Tibetan versions of the Sarvāstivāda monastic code.[^gretil-prmosu] Readers needing such details should consult von Simson's introduction to the edition.
