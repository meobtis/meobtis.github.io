The *Sphuṭārthā* ("Clear Meaning"), also called the *Abhidharmakośavyākhyā*, is a Sanskrit commentary by Yaśomitra on Vasubandhu's *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya*, which is itself a commentary on the verses of the *Abhidharmakośa*. In the opening verses the author says that he has written the work to explain the *Bhāṣya*, and that he draws on the explanations of earlier commentators.[^gretil-sphutartha] The text is arranged in the eight chapters (*kośasthāna*) of the root work, the first being the "Dhātunirdeśa", the exposition of the elements.[^gretil-sphutartha]

Very little is documented about Yaśomitra himself. In 2018 Dominik Wujastyk reported that a date of 580 is given in one reference work but that he could not trace its source, and he asked for scholarship on the author's date, residence and lineage.[^wujastyk2018] I found no reliable source for the work's Tibetan or Chinese reception or for its manuscript tradition, so these are left out here.

Two printed editions underlie the digital texts. Wogihara's Roman-script edition appeared in Tokyo in two parts, chapters 1–3 and 4–8, and was reprinted in 1971; the GRETIL text derives from it.[^gretil-sphutartha][^cinii-wogihara] The DSBC text is the first volume of Narendra Nath Law's edition of 1949.[^dsbc870]
