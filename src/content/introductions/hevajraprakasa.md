The *Hevajraprakāśa* ("Illumination of Hevajra") is a Sanskrit Buddhist text that the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files under *śāstrapiṭaka*, in the category of commentaries on Yoginī tantras (*yoginītantraṭīkā*). Its record gives no author, editor, publisher or year.[^dsbc960]

The opening of the text, whose invocation is marked as omitted, announces that the generation stage (*utpattikrama*) is to be explained. The author says that Jālandharipāda's *Vajrapradīpa* and Bhadrapāda's *Dveṣavajrasādhana* are hard for a person of limited intelligence to put into practice, and that he therefore draws on those works and on the root tantra to combine the generation-stage and completion-stage sādhanas. He cites the *Hevajra* in support of the two stages.[^dsbc960] Beyond this, little reliable information on the work's authorship, date or transmission could be found, and no translations or manuscripts are documented here.
