The *Piṇḍīkrama* is a short Sanskrit Buddhist tantric work in verse, headed in both digital editions as composed by the master (*ācārya*) Nāgārjuna. After an opening verse of homage, the author announces that he will explain the *piṇḍīkrama* on the basis of an understanding of the "six-fold" (*ṣaṭkoṭi*) meanings of the Samāja tantra, that is, the Guhyasamāja, for the benefit of all beings.[^gretil-pindikrama] No established English title could be verified from the sources consulted. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files the work among commentarial literature (*ṭīkā*) on higher yoga tantras.[^dsbc356]

Little further reliable information could be gathered for this introduction. Its relation to the *Pañcakrama* is indicated only by the printed edition, which presents the two works together, and by the GRETIL heading, which describes the text as "transmitted as the first *krama* of Nāgārjuna's *Pañcakrama*".[^gretil-pindikrama] The attribution to Nāgārjuna (a name borne by several authors in the tantric tradition), the date, the commentarial tradition and the Tibetan and Chinese translations are therefore not discussed here, and the reader should consult specialist scholarship on the Guhyasamāja literature.
