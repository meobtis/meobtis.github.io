The *Śālistambakamahāyānasūtraṭīkā* is a commentary on the *Śālistamba Sūtra*, the "Rice Seedling" sūtra, in which Maitreya explains, at Śāriputra's request, a brief teaching the Buddha gave on seeing a rice seedling. The sūtra treats outer and inner dependent arising and the twelve links.[^84000toh210]

Three Indian commentaries on the sūtra survive in Tibetan and Mongolian translation: Kamalaśīla's *Śālistambaṭīkā*, and a *Śālistambakamahāyānasūtraṭīkā* and a *Śālistambakakārikā*, both attributed to Nāgārjuna. Jeffrey Schoening studied and translated them in 1995.[^84000toh210] No complete Sanskrit text of the sūtra itself is known to survive, only quotations and modern reconstructions.[^84000toh210]

The digital text is not a transmitted Sanskrit original. Its opening lines give the Tibetan title and label the text *saṃskṛta - punaruddhāraḥ*, a Sanskrit reconstruction, and it opens with an homage to Mañjuśrī Kumārabhūta, which is why it is catalogued as a Sanskrit text based on a modern edition.[^gretil-salistamba] Little further reliable information on this particular commentary, such as its date or the details of its reconstruction, could be found, and the attribution to Nāgārjuna is the one recorded in the GRETIL catalogue and by 84000.
