# Śālistambasūtram

1.1 śālistambasūtram |  
🚻⨀①🆎(śāli+stamba+sūtra)  
the Sutra of the Rice Seedling  
The Sutra of the Rice Seedling.

1.3 ekasmin samaye bhagavān rājagṛhe viharati sma gṛdhrakūṭe parvate mahatā bhikṣusaṃghena sārdhamardhatrayodaśabhirbhikṣusahasraiḥ saṃbahulaiśca bodhisattvamahāsattvaiḥ |

At one time the `Bhagavān` (the Blessed One) was dwelling in Rājagṛha on Vulture Peak Mountain, together with a great community of monks consisting of twelve thousand five hundred monks and many bodhisattva mahāsattvas.

Then the `āyuṣmān` (venerable) Śāriputra approached the place where the walking path of the `bodhisattva` (awakening-being) `mahāsattva` (great being) Maitreya was.

Having approached and exchanged various kinds of pleasant talk with one another, both of them sat down on a rock-slab.

Then the āyuṣmān Śāriputra said this to the bodhisattva mahāsattva Maitreya: 'Today, having seen a rice stalk here, the Bhagavān spoke this `sūtra` (discourse) to the `bhikṣu` (monks): "bhikṣu, whoever sees `pratītyasamutpāda` (dependent origination) sees dharma."'

Having spoken thus, the Bhagavān became silent.

⏏️(evam) 🚻⨀⑦(ukta) 🚹⨀①(maitreya) 🚹⨀①(bodhisattva) 🚹⨀①(mahāsattva) 🚹⨀②(āyuṣmat) 🚹⨀②🆎(śāradvatī+putra) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(etad) 🟢⨀🤟⏮(bravīti) ⏏️(atra) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(yad) 🚻⨀②(ukta) 🚹⨀⓪(bhadanta) 🚹⨀⓪(śāriputra) 🚹⨀③(bhagavat) 🚹⨀③🆎(dharma+svāmin) 🚹⨀③🆎(sarva+jña) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(yad) 🚹⨂⓪(bhikṣu) 🚹⨀②🆎(pratītya+samutpāda) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati) , ⏏️(sa) 🚹⨀②(dharma) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati)

*️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(yad) 🚹⨀②(dharma) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati) , *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(tad) 🚹⨀②(buddha) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(paśyati) ⏏️(iti) , ⏏️(tatra) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀①(katama) 🚹⨀①🆎(pratītya+samutpāda) ⏏️(nāma)

Whoever sees the teaching, he sees the Buddha; in that regard, what is this so-called pratītyasamutpāda?

🚹⨀①🆎(pratītya+samutpāda) ⏏️(nāma) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(yad) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(idam) *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑦(idam) 🚹⨀⑦🔽(at)(sat) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(idam) 🟢⨀🤟▶️(bhavati) , *️⃣🤟🚹⨀⑥(idam) 🚹⨀⑤(utpāda) *️⃣🤟🚻⨀①(idam) 🔵⨀🤟▶️(utpadyate)
