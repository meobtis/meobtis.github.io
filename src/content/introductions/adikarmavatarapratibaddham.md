The *Ādikarmāvatārapratibaddham* is a short Sanskrit text of mixed prose and verse, classed in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon among the "śāstrapiṭaka" works on a practitioner's chosen deity (*sveṣṭadevatā*). It opens with a salutation to the Blessed Tārā (*bhagavati tāre namastubhyam*), and its closing lines describe it as "connected with" (*pratibaddha*) the *Ādikarmāvatāra*, giving a length of 110 *grantha* units.[^dsbc922]

The text begins by describing how a spiritual friend (*kalyāṇamitra*) should guide people troubled by suffering, sorting them by capacity and leading them to the three refuges and to the aspiration to awakening. It goes on to give a ritual of refuge and offering, with verses on observing the eight-limbed fast on lunar days, offerings to the Buddha, and meditation on beings' welfare and the long endurance of the teaching. It ends with a prayer for all beings' happiness.[^dsbc922]

Little else is documented. The edition's record gives no author, date, printed edition or manuscript, and no scholarly discussion of the title has been found.[^dsbc922]
