The *Subhāṣitaratnakaraṇḍakathā* is a Sanskrit Buddhist work in verse, divided into numbered thematic sections, the first of which is the "Puṇyaprotsāhanakathā" (the exhortation to merit). The GRETIL text counts 27 sections, from the Puṇyaprotsāhanakathā to a final Prajñākathā, followed by a Pāramitāparikathā, a verse summary (*Saṃgrahaślokāḥ*) and a colophon, with 191 numbered verses.[^gretil-srkk]

The title of the printed edition on which the digital text rests describes the work as a late Buddhist text on the doctrine of merit (*Verdienstlehre*).[^gretil-srkk] The colophon attributes the work to Ācārya Śūra (*kṛtir ācāryaśūrasya*); the GRETIL header itself leaves the author field empty and says nothing about identification, date or place of composition.[^gretil-srkk] Little further reliable information on the text, its manuscripts or any Tibetan or Chinese translations could be gathered for this introduction, so questions of authorship and date should be checked in Hahn's edition.
