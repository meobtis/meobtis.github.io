The *Kalpadrumāvadānamālā* ("Garland of Legends of the Wish-Fulfilling Tree") is a Sanskrit collection of avadānas, that is, Buddhist edifying narratives about the past and present deeds of the Buddha, his disciples and other figures. Avadāna literature in Sanskrit includes large compilations such as the *Mahāvastu*, the *Avadānaśataka* and the *Divyāvadāna*.[^wikipedia-avadana] The digital text is in verse and is organised in numbered sections, each with its own verse numbering, cited under the abbreviation KDA.[^gretil-kda]

The portion of the text available here opens with the tenth section, the story of the monk Subhūti (*subhūtyavadāna*). It is framed as a dialogue in which King Aśoka asks the teacher Upagupta for a further edifying account of what the Buddha taught, and Upagupta replies by telling the story.[^gretil-kda] Beyond what the edition itself shows, little reliable information on the work's author, date, place of composition, manuscripts or translations could be found in the sources consulted, so these are left unstated here.
