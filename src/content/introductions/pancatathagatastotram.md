The *Pañcatathāgatastotram* ("Hymn to the Five Tathāgatas") is a very short Sanskrit praise poem of five verses, followed by a colophon. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon files it under the stotra (hymn) section, in the sub-category of the five Buddhas (*pañcabuddha*).[^dsbc164] Each verse opens with an invocation beginning with the honorific *śrī* and a name element: *buddha*, *vajra*, *ratna*, *padma* and *viśva*. These correspond to the five Tathāgatas of the Buddha, Vajra, Ratna, Padma and Karma (*viśva*) families, although the text itself names the families only through these epithets.[^dsbc164]

Little reliable information about the work has been found beyond the front matter of its digital edition. The author, date and place of composition, and any Tibetan or Chinese translation, are not recorded in the sources consulted, and no scholarly study of this particular hymn was located, so none is claimed here. Its source is the printed collection *Bauddha Stotra Saṃgraha*, edited by Janardan Shastri Pandey.[^dsbc164]
