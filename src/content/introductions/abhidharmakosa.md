The *Abhidharmakośa* ("Treasury of the Abhidharma", also called the *Abhidharmakośakārikā*) is the set of verses with which Vasubandhu summarised the Abhidharma, the scholastic analysis of experience, as taught by the Kashmiri Vaibhāṣika school. It is the root text of the *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya*, his own commentary on the verses.[^sep2021] The verses are commonly reckoned at about 600, arranged in eight chapters, to which the commentary adds a ninth, prose chapter.[^tbe2021]

The eight chapters of the verses treat the elements (*dhātu*), the faculties (*indriya*), the world (*loka*), action (*karma*), the latent tendencies (*anuśaya*), the path and the persons who follow it (*mārga*, *pudgala*), knowledge (*jñāna*) and meditative attainments (*samāpatti*).[^gretil-akk] The ninth chapter, found only in the commentary, is an extended defence of no-self titled the "Refutation of the Person".[^sep2021]

Vasubandhu is generally placed in the fourth to fifth century CE, in the Gupta period, although the dating is not settled.[^sep2021] According to the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, the verses present the doctrines of the Kashmiri Vaibhāṣikas, while the commentary frequently takes a Sautrāntika standpoint and criticises them; the commentary became the main source for non-Mahāyāna philosophy in Tibetan and East Asian Buddhist schools.[^sep2021]

The work was translated into Chinese twice: by Paramārtha in the sixth century (Taishō 1559) and by Xuanzang in the seventh (Taishō 1560 and 1558), and other translations and commentaries exist in Tibetan.[^tbe2021] The Sanskrit text of the commentary was long known only through these translations. Rāhula Sāṃkṛtyāyana published a reconstructed Sanskrit edition in 1931, based on Louis de La Vallée Poussin's French translation from the Chinese, and later found a Sanskrit manuscript of the verses and commentary at Ngor monastery in Tibet during his 1934 and 1936 expeditions.[^mejor2024] Printed editions of the Sanskrit commentary, notably Pradhan's, are the basis on which the digital text was prepared.[^gretil-akk]
