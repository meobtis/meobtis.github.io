The *Udānavarga* ("Chapters of Inspired Utterances") is a collection of Dharmapada-type verses arranged by theme in 33 chapters (*varga*), running from the first chapter on impermanence (*anitya*) to the last on the brahmin (*brāhmaṇa*).[^anandajoti-index] It is the largest extant collection of its kind: the edition on which Ānandajoti Bhikkhu's recent translation is based contains 1061 verses, counting variants that cannot be reconciled.[^anandajoti-intro] Its verses, many of them well-known maxims on craving, heedfulness, speech, the path and nirvāṇa, have numerous parallels in the Pali *Dhammapada* (423 verses in 26 chapters) and the Patna *Dharmapada* (414 verses in 22 chapters), and the same translator's concordance traces hundreds of Pali parallels, including some 451 from the *Dhammapada* and 101 from the *Udāna*.[^anandajoti-intro]

The Chinese and Tibetan traditions attribute the work to Dharmatrāta, an Abhidharma master of the Dārṣṭāntika branch of the Sarvāstivāda who is placed around the second century CE. The commentary he is said to have written has not survived, and modern scholarship tends to regard the text as a composite anthology that evolved over time, so that Dharmatrāta may have been its redactor, a commentator, or merely a name lending authority.[^anandajoti-intro] The Sanskrit is described as a Sanskritised form of an earlier Prakrit.[^anandajoti-intro]

The Tibetan version, found in the Kangyur (*mdo sde*, volume *sa*), was translated into English by Rockhill in 1883, and the Chinese version has been translated by Willemen; Peter Skilling has translated the first chapter, and Ānandajoti Bhikkhu's translation of December 2025 is described by its author as the first complete English translation of the Sanskrit.[^anandajoti-intro][^anandajoti-index] The standard critical edition of the Sanskrit is that of Franz Bernhard (Göttingen, 1965), which the digital text here follows.[^anandajoti-intro][^gretil-udanavarga] Further detail on the manuscripts, and on Chinese and Tibetan catalogue numbers, was not found in the sources consulted.
