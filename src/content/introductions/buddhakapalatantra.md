The *Buddhakapālatantra* is a Buddhist tantra classified in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon among the yoginītantras.[^dsbc797] The text available here is not the whole work: the edition it derives from, *The Buddhakapālatantra Chapters 9 to 14*, edited by Luo Hong, covers only those six chapters.[^dsbc797]

The chapters (*paṭala*) are headed, in order, as the chapter on conduct (*caryāpaṭala*), on the rite of bathing with divine great herbs, on the instruction for becoming a *vidyādhara*, on the application of various mantras, on the purification of the mind, and on the request for recitation and the origin of all tantras.[^dsbc797] The opening chapter describes the practitioner's wandering conduct, carrying a skull-bowl (*kapāla*) and begging from house to house.[^dsbc797] Little reliable scholarly information on the work's authorship, date, translations or reception could be consulted in preparing this note, so none is claimed here.
