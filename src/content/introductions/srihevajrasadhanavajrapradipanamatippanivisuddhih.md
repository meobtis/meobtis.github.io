The *Śrīhevajrasādhanavajrapradīpanāmaṭippaṇīviśuddhiḥ* is a Sanskrit prose commentary of the kind called a *ṭippaṇī* (a short explanatory gloss) on a sādhana, a meditative practice text, of the Buddhist tantric deity Hevajra. The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon classes it among the commentaries on the Yoginī tantras.[^dsbc950] No established English title for it was found.

The opening verse pays homage to Hevajra and announces that the work will clarify the sādhana "in accordance with the generation stage" (*utpattikrama*). The commentary then quotes the sādhana text section by section and glosses it, beginning with the preliminaries of worship and the four sublime abodes and moving through the protection circle, the visualisation of the deity and the ritual sequence that follows. In its closing lines it refers to the work it explains as the Hevajra sādhana composed by the ācārya Saroruha, and calls the present text its *vajrapradīpā* "clarification of the commentary". The colophon adds that the work is by the paṇḍita-ācārya Śrī Surata.[^dsbc950]

Nothing further about the author, date or place of composition, or about Tibetan or Chinese translations or manuscripts, could be established from the sources available for this introduction, and the attributions above rest only on the colophon of the digital text. The edition records no printed source, so it is not known whether it derives from a manuscript or a published edition.[^dsbc950]
