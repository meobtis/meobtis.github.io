The *Pratisarākalpadhāraṇī* is a very short Sanskrit text of the dhāraṇī (protective incantation) type. In the digital edition it opens with a verse announcing a "vidyādhara-kalpa", a ritual procedure for the holder of spells, followed by the homage to the Buddha, the Dharma and the Saṃgha and a long string of Sanskrit mantra syllables and epithets addressed to a female deity, and it closes with a colophon calling it the "noble *Pratisarākalpadhāraṇī*".[^gretil-pratisara]

The title points to the cult of Pratisarā (Mahāpratisarā), the "Great Amulet", one of the five protective goddesses of the Pañcarakṣā group. A longer scripture on this figure, preserved in the Tibetan Kangyur (Toh 561), prescribes writing an incantation on an amulet to be worn for protection from illness, harmful spirits and other dangers.[^84000toh561] Whether the present text is an excerpt, a ritual manual or an independent composition related to that scripture could not be established from the sources consulted, and its author, date and place of composition are not documented there. Little reliable information about the text beyond its edition record was found.
