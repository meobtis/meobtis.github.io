The *Lokeśvaraśatakam* ("Hundred Verses on Lokeśvara") is a Sanskrit stotra, a poem of praise, addressed to the bodhisattva Lokeśvara, a name of Avalokiteśvara. In the digital text it opens with a homage to Lokanātha and then praises, in elaborate ornate metres, the radiance of the feet and toenails of the deity; it runs to one hundred numbered verses followed by a short concluding verse in which the poet expresses his devotion to Avalokiteśvara in life after life.[^dsbc127]

The closing colophon names the work as composed by the poet Vajradatta, styled "mahākṣapaṭalika" (a title of office, apparently a record-keeper), and the text's heading likewise ascribes it to Vajradatta-ācārya.[^dsbc127] Little reliable information about the author, the date or place of composition, commentaries, or Tibetan and Chinese translations could be verified in the sources consulted, so none is asserted here. The text is catalogued by the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon under hymns to Avalokiteśvara.[^dsbc127]
