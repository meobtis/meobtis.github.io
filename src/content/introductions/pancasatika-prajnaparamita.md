The *Pañcaśatikā prajñāpāramitā* is a Perfection of Wisdom (*prajñāpāramitā*) sūtra in Sanskrit; the title, "the Perfection of Wisdom in Five Hundred (lines)", indicates a short text of the genre. The digital text opens with a salutation to the Blessed Lady, the noble Perfection of Wisdom, and the usual introduction in which the Buddha is staying at Rājagṛha on Vulture Peak with a large assembly of monks and bodhisattvas. The Buddha then addresses Subhūti, declaring that form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness and the sense-bases are without a real basis. He says that ordinary, foolish people who fail to understand this cling to these phenomena and so remain in cyclic existence, unable to proceed even by the Śrāvaka or Pratyekabuddha vehicles, let alone the Mahāyāna.[^dsbc754]

Little reliable information about the work's date, authorship, manuscripts, Tibetan or Chinese translations, or reception could be found in the sources consulted, and no established English title could be identified. The two digital editions listed here derive from the same printed Sanskrit edition, published in Beijing in 2017 by Li Xuezhu and Fujita Yoshimichi.[^dsbc754][^dsbc755]
