The title *Saddharmasmṛtyupasthānasūtra* may be rendered "the sūtra on the establishment of mindfulness of the true Dharma". The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon (DSBC) files it among the Mahāyāna sūtras of the Sūtrapiṭaka and credits the edition to Daniel M. Stuart.[^dsbc813] The opening of the romanised file is headed "(Second Chapter)", which indicates that the text offered here is a portion of the work and not the whole sūtra.

From the opening lines, the text addresses a meditator (*yogācāra*) who contemplates phenomena (*dharmānupaśyī*), asks how a monk gradually abandons unwholesome states and cultivates wholesome ones, and describes the world as turning in saṃsāra through the sense-objects and sense-faculties. It also recommends delight in solitude in forests and similar places.[^dsbc813] Beyond these front-matter details and the opening of the text, little reliable information about its date, authorship, translations or manuscripts could be verified in the sources consulted, so none is asserted here.
