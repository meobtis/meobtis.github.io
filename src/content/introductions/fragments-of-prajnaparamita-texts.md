This is not a single work but a collection of Sanskrit manuscript fragments of Prajñāpāramitā ("Perfection of Wisdom") literature, assembled from sixteen published sources. The leaves are labelled by their source and folio or plate, for example AṣṭāK for Konow's edition and AṣṭāB for Bidyabinod's. Passages restored by the editors are marked in italics, and the beginning of each manuscript line is marked in bold.[^gretil-fragments]

Judging by the sigla and the opening passage, much of the material comes from Central Asian manuscripts of the *Aṣṭādaśasāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā* (the Perfection of Wisdom in eighteen thousand lines). The first leaf contains a dialogue between Subhūti and Śāriputra on the bodhisattva and samādhi. The file also includes a note on a Sogdian manuscript of the *Prajñāpāramitāhṛdayasūtra*.[^gretil-fragments] Little further information about the individual fragments and their dating was found in the sources consulted, so no further claims are made here.
