The *Sarvadurgatipariśodhanatantra* is a Buddhist tantra of the Yoga class, divided into three chapters that present twelve maṇḍalas in all. In Tibetan it is called *Ngan song sbyong rgyud* and is numbered Toh 483 in the Degé Kangyur; the 84000 project translates the title as "The Tantra Purifying Bad Rebirths", while Skorupski's edition and translation use "Elimination of All Evil Destinies".[^84000toh483][^dsbc730]

Its narrative frame concerns a god, Vimalamaṇiprabha, who falls into the Avīci hell. The Buddha teaches that he can be rescued through rites performed on his behalf, and the tantra then sets out maṇḍala rituals, initiation, the making of images and homa fire offerings, after which the god is restored to heaven. The first chapter centres on the principal maṇḍala of Sarvavid Vairocana, the second mostly on maṇḍalas of worldly deities presided over by Vajrapāṇi, and the third on a Cakravartin maṇḍala and a wrathful maṇḍala. The text is notable for stressing rites performed for the benefit of others, such as the dead, rather than practices of personal cultivation.[^84000toh483]

According to the 84000 introduction, the tantra was composed in Sanskrit around the eighth century and translated into Tibetan soon afterwards, probably in the late eighth century. The colophons name Śāntigarbha and Jayarakṣita as translators, although later Tibetan commentators disagreed about this. It is mentioned in early Tibetan sources and became more influential in Tibet than the later translation, the *Portion of the Practice Purifying Bad Rebirths*. No complete Chinese translation is known, and the 84000 introduction notes that a partial Chinese translation corresponds to the later Tibetan version. The introduction also reports that the Sanskrit is not preserved in Nepal's Newar tradition and that only one Sanskrit version survives, resembling the later translation; Skorupski edited both Tibetan translations and the Sanskrit and published an English translation based mainly on the Sanskrit.[^84000toh483] Little else about the text could be verified from the sources consulted.
