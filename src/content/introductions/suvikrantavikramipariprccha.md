The *Suvikrāntavikrāmiparipṛcchā* ("The Questions of Suvikrāntavikrāmin") is a Mahāyāna sūtra of the Perfection of Wisdom (*Prajñāpāramitā*) class. The Sanskrit text opens by giving itself the title *Suvikrāntavikrāmiparipṛcchā nāma sārdhadvisāhasrikā prajñāpāramitā*, that is, a Perfection of Wisdom text of two and a half thousand verse-units, and is divided into numbered chapters, the first being the introductory chapter (*nidānaparivarta*).[^gretil-svp] In the opening scene the Buddha is at Rājagṛha, in the Bamboo Grove, when the bodhisattva Suvikrāntavikrāmin rises and asks him how a bodhisattva should practise the perfection of wisdom, how that practice is brought to completion, and how the bodhisattva avoids the obstructions of Māra.[^gretil-svp]

A Tibetan version is included in the Kangyur, where it is catalogued as Toh 14 in the Perfection of Wisdom section and titled in English by the 84000 project "The Questions of Suvikrāntavikrāmin".[^84000toh14] Its date, place of composition, Chinese parallels and manuscript sources are not documented in the sources consulted, and little reliable information about its history could be found.
