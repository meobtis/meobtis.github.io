The *Hetubinduṭīkā* is a Sanskrit commentary by Arcaṭa on the *Hetubindu* ("Drop of Logical Reasons"), a treatise by the Buddhist logician Dharmakīrti. According to Frauwallner's reconstruction, summarised in the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Dharmakīrti composed the *Hetubindu* after the *Nyāyabindu* and before the *Vādanyāya*.[^sep-dharmakirti] The text opens with a homage to the Jina and verses in which Arcaṭa praises Dharmakīrti's words and announces that he will analyse the *Hetubindu* for less able readers.[^gretil-sanghavi] It then proceeds as a running commentary on the root text, introducing each passage with bracketed headings in the edition.

The digital texts are derived from the printed edition by Sanghavi and Jinavijaya, which publishes Arcaṭa's work together with the subcommentary *Āloka* by Durvekamiśra.[^gretil-sanghavi] Beyond what the edition's own front matter and the text itself show, little reliable information on Arcaṭa's date, school and the work's transmission could be verified, and it is not described further here.
