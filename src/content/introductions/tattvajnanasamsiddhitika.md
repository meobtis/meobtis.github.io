The *Tattvajñānasaṁsiddhiṭīkā* is a Sanskrit commentary (*ṭīkā*) catalogued in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon under the commentaries on Yoginī tantras.[^dsbc800] No established English title has been found. After a salutation to the blessed Vajravārāhī, its opening verses state that the author will set out a sādhana (practice manual) of Vajravārāhī, and the prose that follows introduces the root work as one by Ācārya Śūnyatāsamādhivajra, whose goddess-sādhana the commentator proceeds to explain verse by verse.[^dsbc800] The text appears to comment on the *Tattvajñānasaṁsiddhi*, a separate work that is also in this index; this identification rests on the title and the shared subject, not on an independent source.

The closing colophon of the text attributes the commentary to the Madhyamaka-following monk Śrījñānacandra, in a section on the dedication of merit (*puṇyapariṇāmanāvidhi*), and ends by wishing that beings, freed of the stains of conflict and defilement, attain perfect awakening.[^dsbc800] The date and place of composition, any Tibetan or Chinese translation, manuscripts and any printed edition could not be established: the DSBC record names no publisher or year, and no independent scholarly source on this commentary was found when this introduction was prepared. Little is therefore documented here beyond what the digital edition itself shows.[^dsbc800][^dsbc799]
