The *Buddhist yoga manual* is a Sanskrit Buddhist text on meditative practice, edited from manuscript fragments belonging to the Turfan finds of Central Asia. It is usually cited as the *Yogalehrbuch* ("YL"), after Dieter Schlingloff's edition *Ein buddhistisches Yogalehrbuch*, published in Berlin in 1964 with a volume of plates in 1966.[^gretil-yoga] The surviving text is highly fragmentary: many passages are broken off, and the digital transcription marks gaps by the number of missing syllables, restored readings in italics and editorial remarks in braces.[^gretil-yoga]

Little reliable information on the work's date, authorship, school, contents and translations could be gathered for this introduction beyond the edition records. A full account of the work's structure and place in Buddhist meditation literature should be sought in Schlingloff's edition and its 2006 reprint, edited by J.-U. Hartmann and H.-J. Röllicke (Buddhismus-Studien 5).[^gretil-yoga]
