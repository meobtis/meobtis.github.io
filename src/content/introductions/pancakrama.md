The *Pañcakrama* ("Five Stages") is a Sanskrit Buddhist tantric work whose digital editions divide it into five sections. The text opens with an invocation of the Buddha and announces the first section, the *vajrajāpakrama* (stage of vajra recitation), which the author says he will set out in accordance with the yoga tantra; the first verses present the practice as a stairway for those who have mastered the generation stage and wish to reach the perfection stage, and connect it with the winds (*vāyu*) that carry consciousness.[^gretil-pancakrama] The Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon edition heads the text as composed by the master Nāgārjuna and files it among commentaries on the higher yoga tantras.[^dsbc355]

Little further reliable information could be gathered for this introduction, and the attribution, date, place of composition, commentarial tradition and Tibetan and Chinese translations of the work are therefore not discussed here. The only printed edition documented in the edition records is Ram Shankar Tripathi's of the *Piṇḍīkrama* and *Pañcakrama*, published at Sarnath in 2001, from which both digital texts derive.[^gretil-pancakrama][^dsbc355]
