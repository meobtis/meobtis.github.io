The *Vajrapradīpā* ("Lamp of the Vajra") is catalogued in the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon among the commentaries on the Yoginītantras, within the section of treatises (*śāstrapiṭaka*).[^dsbc957] The text opens with a homage to Śrī Hevajra and a verse announcing a clarification (*viśuddhi*) of the generation stage (*utpattikrama*), and then lists the terms of a "sādhana sūtra" that it goes on to explain item by item, beginning with the worship circle (*pūjācakra*).[^dsbc957] A heading at the start of the digital file connects it with a Hevajra lineage of Saroruhavajra, but the edition's metadata gives no author, editor or date, so the attribution is not independently documented here.[^dsbc957]

Its subject matter places it among the literature on the Hevajra cycle, which includes the *Hevajra Tantra* itself and several commentaries and sādhanas such as the *Yogaratnamālā* and the *Muktāvalī*.[^wiki-hevajra] Little reliable information about the *Vajrapradīpā* specifically (its authorship, date, Tibetan or Chinese translation, manuscripts or printed editions) could be found in the sources consulted, and none is asserted here.
