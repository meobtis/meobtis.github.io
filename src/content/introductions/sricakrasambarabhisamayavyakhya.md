The *Śrīcakrasambarābhisamayavyākhyā* ("Commentary on the Realisation of Śrī Cakrasambara") is a short Sanskrit commentary in the Yoginī-tantra literature, which the Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon lists among its commentaries on Yoginī tantras (*yoginītantraṭīkā*).[^dsbc735] The text opens with a salutation to Śrī Cakrasambara and then describes the practitioner's meditation: the yogin sits in a high place such as a charnel ground or mountain, explains the four syllables of the name Śrī-heruka, and cultivates the four immeasurables (loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy and equanimity) before the visualisation of the deity and the recitation of mantras.[^dsbc735]

No author, date or place of composition is given in the edition record, and I could not consult scholarly sources on the work, so its attribution, its relation to other Cakrasaṃvara *abhisamaya* texts and any Tibetan or Chinese translations are not documented here. The only named person in the record is the modern editor. The digital texts are drawn from a 2013 printed edition in the *Dhīh* journal of the Central University of Tibetan Studies, Sarnath.[^dsbc735][^dsbc736]
