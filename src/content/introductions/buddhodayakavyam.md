The *Buddhodayakāvyam* is a Sanskrit poem (*kāvya*) on the life of the Buddha. The text opens with the heading "sugatakaviratna-śāntibhikṣuśāstrikṛtaṁ", which attributes it to Śāntibhikṣu Śāstri, styled "jewel among poets of the Sugata". It is divided into cantos called *prasaṅga* (and in one case *sarga*), ten in all, and begins with a first canto titled *janmamaṅgalam*, the auspicious birth.[^dsbc909]

Little reliable information about the work could be found: no scholarly discussion, printed edition record, date or translation was available in the sources consulted. Even the digital edition's bibliographic record lacks a title, editor, publisher or year for the printed source.[^dsbc909] The attribution and the contents beyond the canto headings are therefore stated only as the text itself presents them.
